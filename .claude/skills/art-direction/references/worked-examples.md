## 1. A new direction: "Sky Couriers", a delivery game on floating islands
**Brief.** Third person behind and above, phones first. The player flies parcels between islands; the parcel and the next drop-off must read first; the feeling is "breezy, a little daring". Image cap from the brief: 9.

1. **Moment and problem** (§3 step 1): read-first = the parcel glow and the drop-off ring; constraint = a 800x360 phone at 14 studs; success test = in the phone-size grey of the busiest route capture, the drop-off ring is the strongest local contrast after the HUD.
2. **Subject:** world rule "the post office keeps the sky islands together"; the courier's card "cheerful + reckless"; sell list: 1 flight, 2 delivery, 3 islands as neighbourhoods. Levers: high saturation on accents only, proportions pushed (big heads, big parcels), simple toy-like props, a designed value structure.
3. **Research board:** our anchors: none yet. Real world: post-office signage, kites, harbour cranes, paper-craft. Outside games: 1950s travel posters. Keyword table: "sky" → pale cyan ground colour, cloud rest areas; "courier" → satchel, stamps, a cap; "daring" → steep camera tilt in key art only.
4. **Rough options:** 9 hooks written, the first ("cartoon fantasy islands") marked warm-up. Kept 4: A paper-craft islands; B toy plastic Roblox-native; C cel-shaded postal uniform world; D (radical) night delivery by lantern light. 4 hero roughs + 4 gameplay-view roughs = 8 images, one per chat, medium quality; one regeneration (B's parcel was drawn twice): 9 of the 9-image cap. Looked at: one colour-and-grey contact sheet; the grey row showed D's ring disappearing into the dark sea (value pass failed), so D's gameplay view was opened alone.
5. **Grey and silhouette pass:** `look_check.py value` and `thumb --size 800x360` on each gameplay view; A and B kept the ring as the top local contrast (contrast pairs 3.4 and 3.1), C 1.6 (the uniforms out-contrasted the ring), D 1.2. Black-fills: A and B differ (folded paper edges vs rounded blocks), so they are two directions.
6. **Owner sheet:** A and B, the two that passed, at equal finish, each packet answering the game designer's six concept-art questions (for B: a round-bodied runner; the parcel a third of the courier's height; the ring first at 800x360; plastic and stamped paper; couriers and island keepers; the signature moment is the catch at the ring); under them C (1.6) and D (1.2) as a numbers list with the reason each failed; the goal question "which makes the next drop-off easiest to find at phone size?", recommendation B (best read, cheapest to build in Studio parts), A's paper motif proposed for the UI frames. D is proposed as a closed door ("night as an event, not the base look") and logged only after the owner's answer.
7. **Bible rev 1 (PROPOSAL):** screen order (HUD and drop-off ring first, couriers second, islands as backdrop); value bands (islands 20-80 %, characters 8-92 %); reserved colours (drop-off orange: islands use no orange above saturation 40; a ring shape partner); key light from the upper left at ClockTime 14; shape vocabulary rounded blocks, triangles only on hazards; motion line "light, bouncy, quick to settle".
8. **Cheap proof:** asked the environment designer for one island greybox with 2 lighting variants and the modeler for a courier blockout beside the avatar, before any texture.
9. **Hand-back:** the sheet (2 options) and the numbers list, bible rev 1, the image count (8 made, 1 regenerated, 1 sheet and 1 single image looked at), one weakness ("B's islands are close to stock Roblox: the paper motif must carry identity"), digest item with default B.

## 2. A busy hub: "the shop doesn't read" in a pet game
**Inputs.** Five captures from the studio operator, all at the spawn camera: busiest hub moment; the same view with UI off and with effects off (layer cuts: which layer competes; the UI-off cut also carries the clipped-white count); environment only (UI, effects and characters off together: the band); the lowest quality; plus a mask capture of the egg shop; bible rev 3. The review row "Stage" in `art-review/references/self-review.md` §3 names the lines.

1. **Cold read first** (EV5): from the phone-size grey of the busiest capture, before the brief: "fountain, then the trees, then the shop". Intended: the shop first.
2. **Measure:** on the environment-only capture, the play area's p5-p95 is 0.012-0.86 luminance, wider than bible rev 3's band (0.020-0.69), and the near-black count finds 0.8 % of the play area in pure black under the arcade roofs (RD3, `look-check.md` §5 lines 8-9); on the UI-off cut, below the horizon with the named accents excluded, the fountain spray holds 1.9 % clipped white (RD5; on the busiest capture the HUD icons and the sun would have counted too). Contrast pairs on the squint view of the busiest capture: fountain 4.2, shop 2.1 (RD2). Reserved danger red share on the environment-only capture, gameplay objects excluded: 2.8 % (flower beds and banners) against a target near 0 (CL1).
3. **Rank:** F1 the environment band is too wide (the hierarchy depends on it); F2 the fountain is the brightest spot; F3 decoration uses the reserved red; what works: the shop's silhouette is the tallest shape and clearly unique.
4. **Direct:** F1 and F2 to the environment designer as outcomes ("bring the hub into the environment band; the shop becomes the strongest local contrast at the spawn camera"), with one generated paint-over of capture c01 (the worst note; the brief's cap and batch yes allow it) lowering the spray and darkening the plaza edges toward the shop; F3 as "decoration takes no reserved hue" with the marked-pixel image of the reserved share as its picture (no generation), the craft left open. Acceptance: the same five captures, RD2-RD5 and CL1 re-run.
5. **Hand-back:** verdict revise (rule: bible rev 3 bands), the ranked table, cohesion table, 5 captures measured, 2 looked at (busiest and the grey squint), 1 paint-over generated, could-not-check: the fountain's motion at normal speed (owner on the playable preview).

## 3. A variant: the "Molten Core" hammer
**Inputs.** The base hammer's anchors in the bible (oversized square head, glowing core slot, wrapped grip); the variant's reference image and the modeler's flat-key capture at the game camera; the game design table's footprint band.

1. **Anchors** (ID1): all three present in the variant's black-fill; the core slot is hidden in the swing key pose but visible in idle: accepted (one may hide in a pose).
2. **Footprint** (ID5): black-fill area of base 0.071, variant 0.076 of the frame at the same camera and distance (+7 %, inside the 10 % band); IoU 0.81 reported as change, not quality.
3. **Brightness** (ID5): the molten glow's top values sit above the combat hit flash in the capture: a finding to the VFX designer ("cosmetic glow below combat-effect brightness").
4. **Variant or same?** (ID3): a new version, so a new palette. The lava colour is checked against the reserved colours (CL2, with `art-review/references/look-check.md` §5 line 4 run on the flat render): this game reserves its saturated red for danger zones, and the image's hottest cracks measure within the reserved swatch's distance, so the molten treatment moves toward orange-gold; the two options go to the owner at the same camera.
5. **Likeness** (ID2): the shop icon render uses the same head angle and core slot as the base icon.
