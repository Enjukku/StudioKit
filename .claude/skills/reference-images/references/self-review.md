# Self-review lines for reference images

## 1. How to run it
- **Inputs.** Each line names its input: the brief file, the manifest, the assembled prompt (`art_refs.py print`), the generated image, its black-fill or grey (§4), the contact sheet, the art bible, or a partner at a named check-in. A line whose input is missing is NOT REVIEWED, with what would close it (could-not-check list).
- **Light checks** (owner J005-D8). Numbers for every image; open images only for a failing or borderline number, the contact sheet, the pick and each final once. Count images generated, regenerated, measured and looked at.
- **Screening, not verdicts.** Every number in §4 is a screening aid with its known false readings listed; a failing or borderline number is checked by eye on the image before any fix note or regeneration. Starting values are PROPOSAL; the art bible's or the owner's value wins.
- **You see stills.** Motion, timing feel and how a model will look in play are judged later on captures by the builders and the art director; an image line never claims them.
- **Order: big to small.** Gameplay read and shape first, then value, then colour and light, then detail and finish; stop at the first failing layer and fix it before detail lines. Labels: `measured` (a number), `looked at` (your eye on the named image), `partner-confirmed`, `owner-confirmed`, `not verified`.

## 4. Commands, their silent thresholds and known false readings
Run from the repo root; `<d>` = a `diag` folder beside the images. Each is a screening aid: read its false readings before trusting a number, and look at the named image before a fix.
| ID | Command | Silent thresholds | Known false readings (tested) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C1 | **Black-fill and count:** `python tools/art/look_check.py silhouette <img> --key-tol 16 --out-dir <d> --out <d>/<id>.silhouette.json`; read `component_count` and the components' `area_frac`, then `<d>/<id>.silhouette.png`. Magenta backgrounds: default `--key-tol` | key auto needs 90 % of the border within the tolerance, else `invalid-mask`; components under 0.5 % of the image are not listed; a mask under 0.1 % or over 98 % is `invalid-mask` | at the default tolerance 40 a light part (a white collar on a light-grey backdrop) drops out and one figure counts as 2; at 16 a soft ground shadow becomes its own wide, flat component; touching poses merge (3 poses counted 2); on dark VFX sheets glow halos merge frames or a faint smoke frame vanishes (3 frames counted 2 at 16 and at 40); a strong gradient or vignette gives `invalid-mask` at 16 (re-run at 40 and look at the black-fill); a radial falloff whose centre is more than about 16 levels lighter than the border keys the light centre as subject with no error and the count stays 1 (centre 236 to border 206: a 301 px wide figure measured 443 px; 240 to 200: 589 px; 232 to 220: right), so look at the black-fill once per image before C2, C5, C12 or FN14 use its box; full scenes always give `invalid-mask` (loud) |
| C2 | **At the size players see it:** scale = on-screen subject height ÷ the subject's `bbox_px.h` from C1; `python tools/art/look_check.py thumb <img> --key-tol 16 --size <round(W*scale)>x<round(H*scale)> --out-dir <d> --out <d>/<id>.thumb.json`; look at the small image enlarged (the `-sheet.png` right panel, or the real-size contact sheet, C6), never at 1:1 | writes the small image and a sheet; its silhouette gives area and box, no component count | an on-screen height guessed instead of taken from the game profile or a game-camera render; a scene without `--mask` gives `invalid-mask` (subject numbers missing, small image still written) |
| C3 | **Grey:** `python tools/art/look_check.py value <img> --key-tol 16 --out-dir <d> --out <d>/<id>.value.json`; the luminance image is the grey; read `subject_ring_ratio` and both medians | the ratio is (brighter + 0.05) ÷ (darker + 0.05): never below 1, never says which side is brighter | on a flat-ground image the ring is the backdrop, whose grey the image model picks per image, so the ratio is "against the backdrop", not against the game's ground, and is information only (RO5 uses C12): the same approved character read 2.82 on a white backdrop and 1.41 on mid-grey; a cream pet on light grey read 1.09 and the same pixels on green ground 4.17; a dark subject always passes; a scene needs a traced hero mask (`marketing-craft/references/image-pipeline.md` §4, checked on its overlay: a coarse polygon gives your drawing, not the image) |
| C4 | **Mirror:** the mirror and grey command in `marketing-craft/references/image-pipeline.md` §4 (writes `<id>.mirror.png` and `<id>.grey.png` beside the image); look at the mirror copy as fresh eyes on the pick and each final; on front or side views only, `look_check.py silhouette <img> --key-tol 16 --compare <id>.mirror.png` and read `compare.iou` | alignment scales both to one box height and centres them | a three-quarter view of a fully symmetric design is not mirror-symmetric in the image (real three-quarter references 0.74-0.88; a symmetric figure 1.0 from the front, 0.76 turned), so the number cannot show asymmetry under the three-quarter locks; a small asymmetric element that widens the box drops the IoU far more than its area (one arm and a sword: 0.23; a shoulder block 0.46); read only "symmetric or not" |
| C5 | **Two black-fills:** `look_check.py silhouette <a> --key-tol 16 --compare <b>`; `compare.iou` | aligned by box height and centred: size and position differences are ignored | a recolour of the same option gives 1.0 (same direction: correct); two options of very different size can score high; read IoU as shape change only |
| C6 | **Contact sheet:** `art-review/references/look-check.md` §5 line 6 (colour row over grey row at one height). The **real-size sheet** runs it on the C2 small images at a height of 360: each rough enlarged from its real size, so the sheet shows only what survives at real size | a viewing aid; the grey row is not the measured luminance | mixed aspects: narrow images are padded grey to the widest cell (tested); run on full-size images it shows detail players never see |
| C7 | **3-value study (scenes):** `marketing-craft/references/image-pipeline.md` §4 | default cuts 85 and 170 in grey levels; prints the shares and the share near a cut | above about 0.1 near a cut, groups may split into speckle: re-run with the cuts moved about 15 levels |
| C8 | **Palette roles:** `python tools/art/look_check.py palette <img> --key-tol 16 --k 6 --roles '<bible roles JSON>' --out-dir <d> --out <d>/<id>.palette.json` | no verdict without `--max-delta-e` and `--tolerance-source` | a soft ground shadow keyed in at 16 becomes a subject cluster (8 % of the subject in the test); k-means splits a shaded material into several clusters, so a role's `nearest_cluster` share alone under-reads it (main 0.195 where its hue family summed to 0.525): add the shares of one hue family, judged on the swatches image; a shaded light colour (cream) can cluster as a grey-brown that looks like another family; lit colours drift from the authored swatch |
| C9 | **Icons at real size:** `python tools/art/thumb_check.py icon <img> --out-dir <d> --out <d>/<id>.icon.json` (150x150); item icons at the UI brief's size: `look_check.py thumb <img> --size 48x48` | `component_count` at the small size ignores pieces under 0.5 % of the frame | a full-bleed icon has no flat border: auto key finds no subject, use a mask |
| C10 | **Reserved hue on a scene image:** `art-review/references/look-check.md` §5 line 4, with the gameplay objects that may wear the hue excluded by a traced mask | the CIEDE2000 distance comes from the bible | characters or effects that legitimately wear the hue count as decoration unless excluded; an exclusion mask that misses part of the object leaves that part counted (a coarse hero polygon missed the arms: share 0.0020 left of 0.0070) |
| C11 | **The assembled prompt:** `python tools/art/art_refs.py [--bible <path>] print <manifest>` from the repo root, input paths relative to it | refuses an id outside `[a-z0-9_-]`, an unknown family, an empty identity, a missing input file | an identity that contradicts its lock prints with no error (a back view under a three-quarter lock): read the lock text under every identity |
