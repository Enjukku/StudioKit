# Twelve published cases, as alternatives per design question

The cases do not disagree about one formula; they serve different progression jobs (01:KX1, O). Use them to name options for the owner, never as numbers to copy.

## 1. Pick by design question
| Question | Case | What it shows (V/E) | Transfer hypothesis (O) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Must a new run beat the previous best? | idle prestige (1-3) | lifetime-based prestige keeps paying for repeat runs (less each time); maximum-based pays nothing for an equal maximum | choose the behaviour, not just a root exponent |
| Where should exponential costs live? | Factorio (4) | optional infinite research for very large factories, after finite goals | voluntary surplus sinks, not the gate to ordinary content |
| What survives a fresh character? | Diablo IV (5), WoW (6) | separate journey, catch-up and challenge tracks; max-merge shared reputation | define aggregation (max, sum, per character) before catch-up |
| Why does a generous track feel empty? | Hearthstone (7) | complaints about gaps between bonus rewards; total and spacing both changed | measure gaps between usable rewards, not the total |
| Where does account power meet ranked play? | Clash Royale (8) | stepped level caps by league with the upgrade resource supply | move resource supply and caps together |
| What does a level mean? | LoL (9), TFT (10), Overwatch (11) | mastery as accomplishment; levels as composition choices; flat cosmetic dedication | tell apart skill, options, dedication and combat power |
| Must an unlock be a number? | Mario Wonder (12) | badges change movement; one adds challenge on purpose | movement verbs and voluntary challenge |

## 3. Unknowns (01:U1, O)
No verified enjoyment uplift, churn effect size or universal minutes to first prestige comes from these cases. They are public rules, development accounts and patch notes: they cannot show cause or population-wide satisfaction.
