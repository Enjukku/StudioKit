## 3. How to use a case in a proposal
1. Name the mechanism in one line and why it fits this game.
2. Quote the number only with its reporter, unit and window, and the line "reported result, not a forecast".
3. Say what would show the mechanism works here (the analyst's metric) and what it costs to try.
4. Licensed cases (SpongeBob, Gabby's Dollhouse) never justify using a franchise, character, music or logo (`policy-rights.md` §4).
