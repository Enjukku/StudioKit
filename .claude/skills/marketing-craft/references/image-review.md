# Image self-review: what I check before any image leaves me

> Author: J018 maker marketing · claude-opus-5-5 · high · 2026-10-22. Part of `marketing-craft`; built also from the owner's Riot transcripts (J018). After the stage 3 review: MR-33 (generation faults) added, MR-08 judged only on a real outline, the symbol, keyword-row, variant-palette, never-stretch and content-maturity clauses added. The standards behind each line live in `marketing-design` §4 (M-rows) and §7 (core principles); the stages that produce the inputs are `image-pipeline.md` §1; the commands are `image-pipeline.md` §2 and §4. The per-image evidence form stays `templates/marketing/thumbnail-icon-checklist.md` (I1-I14): where a line below repeats an I-item it says so and the template holds the record.

## 1. How to run it (light checks)
- **Numbers for every candidate, images only where they earn it.** Every candidate and every crop gets its `thumb_check.py` JSON. I look at **one image per candidate: its contact sheet** (master, tile 1:1 with the title, grey, mask), plus the overlay of each newly traced mask once (`image-pipeline.md` §2: a mask I did not check on its overlay is my drawing, not the image). Extra images (mirror, 3-value study, grid, layer toggles, side-by-side with anchors) are made **only for the recommended option**, and for any candidate whose number or sheet fails. The owner sees all options at once on one sheet at tile size.
- **Which lines run when:** MR-01 and MR-02 once per brief; MR-03 to MR-05 once per round; each stage's lines (the Check column of `image-pipeline.md` §1) on every candidate and crop of that stage; MR-28 to MR-31 on the render and the master; the full list once on the recommended final.
- **I see stills.** I judge shape, value, colour, layout and truth. The half-second read on a real phone, appeal and "do players want this" are the owner's call at G1/G2 on the packet; the art director's cold read (tile first, brief second) is the second opinion at review. I never write "reads instantly" as a result: I write what I named from the tile, and who confirmed it.
- **Label each line** by what proved it: `measured` (tool JSON), `looked` (my look at the named image), `art-director`, `game-designer`, `owner-confirmed`, or `not verified` with the missing input. End with the could-not-check list.
- **Order of fixing:** the worst failing line first, one named problem per pass, by layer (brief → options → silhouette → value → light → colour → composition → detail → production); after a fix, re-run every line of that layer and the layers after it.

## 3. The big read at tile size (per candidate; its contact sheet)
| ID | Pass when | Input (budget) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MR-33 | First, once per generated image: no generation faults. Hands and fingers whole and the right count; object and character counts match the brief; no stray letters, numbers, glyphs or fake logos; no merged, doubled or missing parts (a pickaxe fused to the hand, a third foot). A fault is a regeneration with the same identity (at most 2, then report), never a new option. Checked before MR-06 so no number is read on a broken image | the full-size master (1 look) |
| MR-06 | From the tile preview with the title under it, I name the hero and the action in one sentence, and that sentence matches the premise; the grey panel gives the same answer. Same as checklist I1-I2 | the sheet (1 image) |
| MR-07 | One clear hero: with a traced (overlay-checked), keyed or magenta-pass hero mask, `one_clear_moment.small.component_count` is low and `largest_share_of_subject` high (read, no pass number unless an approved tolerance exists); `subject_share_of_frame` matches the size of the hero I traced (a thumbnail hero near 0.04 that reads 0.96 is an inverted mask: redraw; an icon hero may fill most of the frame, so there the mask panel decides). Never read `component_count` from `--box` runs or a coarse polygon: it counts my boxes or polygons. The line names its mask source | JSON; the mask panel for icons |
| MR-08 | Black-fill of the image's real outline: only a mask that follows the head, every limb, the signature item and the headgear (traced in parts and checked on its overlay, or the magenta hero pass of route c) can pass or fail this line; a coarse polygon cannot. On it: the outline names the subject and its mood (sharp reads dangerous, round friendly, square stable); the 3-4 identifying shapes from the bible's identity anchors are visible as chunky shapes, the signature item and headgear break the outline, nothing the message needs is a sliver or a notch; a group reads as one shape (ringed by smaller threats when danger is the point). The line names its mask source | the sheet's mask panel at tile size + the mask's overlay (once per traced mask) |
| MR-09 | The pose: one line of action through the hero, at the peak of a real action or just before it; the weight sits over the feet unless off-balance is the point; a heavy thing is held close to the body, a light one far; no limb points at the lens; an airborne hero tucks the legs into a V at the apex instead of dangling them; I name the premise's adjectives from the pose alone; a comic pose shows its contradiction with the whole body, not only the face; the camera height matches the hero's status (low for dominant, eye level for friendly); a character hero uses the signature move's key pose from the animator (`look_check.py silhouette <still> --mask <hero mask> --compare <key-pose black-fill>`: the IoU and the difference recorded with its reason; a held item or an effect inside the hero mask lowers it) | the master once + the animator's key-pose black-fill |
| MR-10 | Face and identity: the hero's face, colours and anchors match the approved anchor images of the version shown, at the same size (a skin or event variant: its own anchors and palette; the base character in a new scene: the base palette, as the brief records); `look_check.py palette <image> --mask <hero mask> --roles` with the shown version's signature colours from its anchors: every role lands near a cluster (`delta_e2000` under about 10 as a start); a face that drifts is fixed before anyone else sees the image (it draws the first note every time) | side-by-side with anchors (1 image, recommended option); palette JSON |
| MR-11 | Scale: a giant subject has a familiar-size object or the player next to it; the size ratio is obvious in the black-fill of the traced mask | the sheet |

## 4. Value, light and colour
| ID | Pass when | Input (budget) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MR-12 | Contrast is spent on the hero: `contrast.small.subject_ring_ratio` separates hero and surround, the near-white and the most saturated pixels sit only on small focal spots, and the direction is the planned one (hero `p50` above the ring's when the hero is the lit area, below when it is a dark shape on light); `whole_p95_p5_ratio` is wide for a dramatic moment, narrower for a calm or cosy game, as the brief says. Same evidence as checklist I3 | JSON; the grey panel when a number is low or the direction is wrong, and once for the recommended option to see where the near-white sits |
| MR-13 | Value plan: the 3-value study (fixed cuts, `image-pipeline.md` §4) shows the planned groups (dark foreground frame, the hero holding the widest range, a quiet close-valued background in one group) and its printed shares match the plan and the brief's tone; a "near a cut" share above about 0.1 is re-run with moved cuts before any group is judged; after colour, the grey of the colour version, and of each colourway, keeps at least the green-lit `subject_ring_ratio` (same mask); the read still works with glow and effects layers off | 3-value study of the recommended option (1 image); JSON of green-lit vs colour |
| MR-14 | Light: one key direction for the whole image, motivated by the scene (sky, sun, the glowing crack); all highlights face one way; every main form shows a light side and a shadow side (a flat form reads as a sticker); the hero's key edges are crisp and organic transitions soft; a rim separates the hero where it meets a similar-value background; light from below only for a threat; 2-3 light options were compared on the rough before any detail | green-light record; option sheets (numbers for all, look at the chosen and any failing one) |
| MR-15 | Colour roles: `look_check.py palette` (with `--mask` for the hero), clusters summed by hue family by me (k-means splits gradients), lands near the dominant, support and accent shares of the bible's palette roles (about 60/30/10 when the bible has no row); the brand colour is present; gameplay colour meanings are kept: for each of the bible's reserved colours, the reserved-colour share (`image-pipeline.md` §2) outside the things that carry that meaning is near 0 and on the hero 0 for the enemy colour, every marked spot looked at (a `palette` cluster never stands in for this count); saturated pops sit only on focal spots; shadows shift hue instead of going grey | JSON; the master once; the marked image when a share is above 0 |
| MR-16 | Rebalance: after colour, effects and light changes, the read order (MR-17) and MR-12 hold again; smoke, sparks and glow never cover the face or the focal point; the effect-free version also passes; soft smoke or airbrush in a clean style appears only as a format exception in the bible's tier and format exceptions row; a background pattern layer stays only if the read order is the same with it on | sheets of the colour version and the effect-off version (2 images) |
| MR-17 | Read order: the tile shows primary (hero and action), secondary (consequence or stakes), tertiary (place) in that order; 2-3 committed flow lines run through the focal point; small accents sit on the eye path, smaller and quieter than the focus; detail is clumped at the focal points with calm areas between; a busy background reads as texture behind the hero, never as a second subject | the sheet + one look at the master |
| MR-18 | Colour on a phone: the export carries an sRGB profile or none (treated as sRGB); the owner looks at the G2 or G3 packet on a phone (named check-in) | profile read (short command); owner |

## 5. Composition, crops and the grid
| ID | Pass when | Input (budget) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MR-19 | Depth groups: a darker foreground element frames the hero and can be removed; the midground carries the hero and most of the care; the background takes the global changes; in the layered file, foreground off still reads | layer list; one toggle render (route c or layered master) |
| MR-20 | Calm room: a quiet, low-detail area behind the title and around the hero; no accidental gap or bare zone; sibling shapes step in size (big, medium, small), no two equal; no machine-made sameness (identical repeated elements, flat single-colour fills, a tidy symmetry the game does not have); dark masses balanced where planned (the image does not tip to one side in grey). A remove-one-element test: an element whose removal does not lower the read stays removed | grey panel; mirror (MR-24) |
| MR-21 | Bottom band and corners: `bottom_band.subject_share_in_band` near zero with the band measured from the surfaces capture (same as I9 for the Home tile); nothing that carries the message sits in a corner of an icon | JSON; sheet |
| MR-22 | Crop family: every crop in the plan is cut from the master with the crop command (never stretched: its box has the destination's aspect) and passes at its own real size (icon 150x150, the tile from `surfaces.md`, each other destination from its source); each crop's 1-2 anchors sit inside its safe area (inside the circle for round crops); the safe square was marked before the rest was composed | `thumb_check.py` per crop with the crop of the hero mask (numbers for all; sheets for the icon and any failing crop) |
| MR-23 | The grid: the tile placed among the competing tiles of the genre (the trend scout's dated study set) and our own other live images, at tile size: it stands apart, reads, and copies no one's layout, character or palette | one grid image (short command) |
| MR-24 | Fresh eyes: the mirrored copy shows no slanted horizon, tilted verticals or lopsided balance that habit hid; then the grey copy once more | mirror of the recommended option (1 image) |

## 6. Truth, identity and brand
| ID | Pass when | Input (budget) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MR-25 | Truth: every action, scale, reward and rarity shown is what ordinary play gives (no rarer-looking tier, no reward burst for a result the action did not earn, no fake UI); the moment is reachable from the game camera; key art is labelled key art. Same as checklist I5, I7, I8, I14; the game designer confirms at review | claim id + capture; game-designer |
| MR-26 | Family and brand: next to the live icon, the other thumbnails and an in-game capture at the same size it reads as the same game; the title treatment keeps the brand fonts and lock-up and changes one property for an event; any motif comes from the game's own UI, items or story and repeats at several scales across the kit (the logo, a frame, a detail in the key art). The message's symbol reads alone: traced as a black shape, cropped square with the crop command, `thumb_check.py icon <symbol square> --key auto`: one main piece (`component_count`), and I name the symbol from the 150×150 tile on its sheet (the numbers do not see a sliver; the tile does) | side-by-side made with the grid command (1 image); the symbol sheet (1 image, at the green light); art-director |
| MR-27 | Theme and tone: the image reads as this game's genre and tone (a darker twist keeps the hero's appeal); a hidden story detail is invisible at tile size and findable at full size; nothing is a sensitive act that a prop motif could carry instead; no sexualised pose, costume or body; blood, violence and gore in every layer, the foreground first, stay within the game's content maturity label (uploaded images can be moderated whatever the label says) | sheet + master; `platform-policy` for any mature theme (it wins) |

## 7. Production and hand-back
| ID | Pass when | Input (budget) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MR-28 | Frozen big shapes: the render keeps the green-lit hero in the same place and shape: frame IoU of the render's hero mask against the green-lit mask (`image-pipeline.md` §4), recorded. Starting value about 0.8 (two hand-drawn masks of the same hero land near 0.8-0.9); below it, I look at the two mask panels side by side: a moved or reshaped big shape is a new green light, not a polish note. Do not use the aligned `--compare` IoU here: one longer weapon tip shifts its alignment and drops the number | JSON; mask panels only when below the value |
| MR-29 | Final resolution: the asset record gives the master's px size against the destination's ideal (thumbnail 1920×1080, icon at least 512×512) and how it was made (rendered at size, or the largest crop of the model's output); `master.*.meets` true is not enough (the tool cannot see an upscale); any upscale is recorded as one and one crop region is looked at 1:1 | JSON + route record; one 1:1 crop look |
| MR-30 | Layered master: foreground, midground, background, effects, extra light and title are named, toggleable layers (or Blender collections); a text-free version exists; every named variant is made by toggling only | layer or collection list |
| MR-31 | Record: route (a, b, c), prompt records and inputs, `.blend` and camera, every sheet and JSON with its revision, the crop plan, and the closed directions with the reason each lost | campaign record |
| MR-32 | Honest hand-back: each line above labelled by what proved it; the could-not-check list (step, missing input, the capture or check that would close it); every reviewer note mapped to done or questioned with the reason, and the problem behind it re-checked, not only the requested change; the fix log shows one named problem per pass, its layer and the lines re-run after it; at least one named weakness and the next iteration; images generated and sheets looked at, against the stage budgets | hand-back |
