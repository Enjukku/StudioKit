# Outside trends: what moves into Roblox, and how to tell

## 1. The chain
Outside release → repeat creators and remixes → search curiosity → Roblox search and quick prototypes → lasting play → (sometimes) money and retention signals (N2 §1 O). It runs both ways: Roblox play makes clips that grow the outside meme. An earlier outside date is needed for a lead signal; it does not prove cause.

**Five dates, never merged:** announced · released · first observed · broke out · peaked. Write which one each date is.

| Verity (Minecraft horror character) | Web series by YouTube channel ThatMob premiered 2026-05-30 (V 3P, CS-03); fan song by Horror Skunx 2026-07-10, 12-17 million views in about a month (two secondary pages disagree, CS-04); a "Verity Roblox game" video dated 2026-07-25 (one third-party record, CS-05) | Roblox adaptation was visible within 56 days of the series and 15 days of the song (DER, upper bounds) | that Verity is a child creator (it is a character); a game launch lag; that it is still early |
| Bruno Mars concert in Steal a Brainrot | 2026-01-17; Guinness record 12,862,161 concurrent viewers (V OFF record, CS-06) | an event inside an existing hit can activate its installed players | any lasting retention or revenue lift |
| PC game → Roblox | a 2024 guide calls *a dusty trip* Roblox's answer to *The Long Drive* (3P, unchecked date); *Dead Rails* shares the expedition-survival structure (O) | design migration happens | a fixed "PC leads Roblox by N weeks" |

No universal lead time exists in this evidence. Every lag above is a single case and an upper bound on visibility, not a forecast.

Channel roles (N2 §2 O): TikTok and Reels show remixable hooks; YouTube shows formats that last and play that explains itself; Twitch shows live spectator appeal; Discord and Reddit show their own audiences only. No single audience stands for all Roblox players.

## 4. Signals that count
- **Independent creators**, not one viral upload: count distinct channels and the top channel's share (N2 §5 O).
- **Speed, not stock:** views added per hour on a fixed cohort; index rise over equal windows; new derivatives this week.
- **Persistence:** the signal holds across 2+ windows and 2+ independent platforms.
- **Roblox adaptation evidence:** named universe IDs already using the theme, with their own history. None yet → the window may be open; many already → the early window is closed.
- **Events and releases** (shows, films, game launches, music drops, holidays) from official calendars, with the date kind.

## 5. Rights travel with the trend
A trend's popularity grants nothing. Characters, names, songs, sounds, art and catchphrases belong to someone. The test is in `copying-and-rights.md`: borrow the **abstract behaviour or tension** (for example "a helpful companion turns unreliable"), never the character, name, song or scenes. Music: recording, composition, remix and in-game use are four different rights. Creators are tracked by exact channel ID; never collect viewers' data or guess their ages (N2 §2 O).
