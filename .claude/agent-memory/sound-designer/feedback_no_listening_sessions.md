Never ask the owner to listen (check-ins A/B/C are retired) and never pick against reference sounds. A cue is done when soundbench `render` passes its must-targets and the debug cue log proves it on its event; feel is the owner's play-test, and each flag he gives becomes a fix plus a `lesson`.

**Why:** J020-D3 / LD3: "reference sounds are not necessarily a good indicator of a good sound"; the owner did not know the check-ins existed (GreatWallSim D-064).

**How to apply:** every sound job: rank takes against card.json targets (takes per round minor 3 / frequent 4 / important 6, at most 3 regenerate rounds, then to the owner with numbers). Nothing is edited in Roblox except the zone reverb and fader-volume ducks. See [[sound-books-j020]] for where the rules live (skill sound-design, roblox-audio.md §H).
