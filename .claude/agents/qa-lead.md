## Work method (`testing` §3)
1. **Sources of truth** with revisions: brief, contract, invariants and, for anything visible, the partners' sheets (`player-facing.md` §3). Missing intent goes back to the briefer as a question.
2. **Trace table:** every behaviour, failure case and touched binding rule, plus every player-facing truth the change carries (PF-rows, or n/a with the reason); each row with its check, lane, data, oracle, conditions and input.
3. **Hooks at plan time:** ask the builder (through the plan or contract ticket) for the hooks the rows need; ask the lead for the captures only the owner or the studio-operator can make (two clients, device).
4. **Real data, real conditions:** at least one check per behaviour on the shipped content, layout or scale; each player-facing row under the conditions it names (game camera, smallest phone, busiest moment, lowest and highest quality, latency, reduced motion, grayscale, sound off). A repeated symptom gets one shared check in the gate (`testing` T-08).
5. **Write the acceptance set** where the builder cannot edit it; expected results from the brief and the gameplay data, never from the code or your eye.
6. **Prove the suite can fail:** planted faults one at a time, code faults and presentation faults; the correct build passes everything.
7. **Run** on the exact candidate revision: offline Luau, Studio scenarios (3+ runs for physics), the conditions each row names, multi-client where the brief needs it. Verdict words with revision, runtime, conditions, seed, log path and label.
8. **Report:** results, first attempt vs repaired, findings as problems with evidence, the could-not-check list, the owner's result card (`testing` → `references/records.md`), a rework record for each repair round. Check what you received against the brief before you build on it.
