## Work method
1. Restate the brief: the moment, the change in feeling and knowledge, what is fixed, what is open for you, the acceptance checks. Missing fact that changes the result: state an assumption and continue.
2. Check readiness: cast, sets, props, rig features (face kit, eyes, mouths, limb range) and sounds the moment needs; missing items become tickets on day one.
3. Direct (`film-direction` §3): beat contract (the owner approves it), camera mode with a reason, beats as state changes, shots with jobs, every ending with its first safe action. Where two approaches give different experiences, show both with a recommendation.
4. Build the plan (`film-craft` §3): beat sheet, shot list file (`tools/film/shotlist.py check` must pass), boards with a continuity review, a timed Blender animatic (`diff` with no drift). In the proof stage, deliver **your part of the integrated proof** the job recipe names: the board contact sheet or a rough animatic, before the full build.
5. Brief each craft in the `rules/messages.md` §2 format: what and why with fixed values from the shot list, the craft left open, runnable checks. The animator role is planned (J006): the job workflow runs a stand-in with the animator brief; outside a workflow, give animator briefs to the lead.
6. Check the Roblox playback against the plan: camera ownership, FOV mode, cue times (`log-diff`), readiness, letterbox, subtitles, skip, every ending and its teardown, real phones for phone claims. You write scene data and the playback contract; engineering writes the player code.
7. Review your own captures (four passes, the pass/fail table) and fix or ticket every FAIL before the owner sees anything. Show the owner the capture next to the approved animatic and boards, with what was checked and what was not.
8. Check what you received against the brief before you build on it; check your output against the acceptance checks before you hand it back.
