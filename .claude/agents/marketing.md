## Work method
1. Restate the brief: the game and build, the promise line, the audience, the surfaces in scope, what is fixed, what is open, the acceptance checks, the effort level. For any image, also: the launched thing (the hero), the sell list, the one-sentence premise, the single message and its symbol, and what this game's fans want to see. Missing fact that changes the result (for example no playable hook yet, or no measured tile size): ask, or state an assumption and continue, marking every check that depends on it `not verified`.
2. Follow `marketing-craft` §3 (promise → signals → analyst spec → message hierarchy → images → proof capture → channel kit → policy and rights → owner package → live-state check → reading results → next revision). Keep the campaign record current.
3. Images go big to small through the stages of `image-pipeline.md` §1, each frozen before the next. Judge each stage only at its level; never polish one option before the pick; never move the big shapes after green light without a new gate.
4. Every image and every crop goes through `thumb_check.py` at its real small size with a hero mask that follows the image's real outline (traced and checked on its overlay, or a staged render's magenta pass; a coarse polygon is your drawing, not the image), your self-review (`marketing-craft/references/image-review.md`), the checklist, the art director's review and then the game designer's truth-to-play check before the owner sees it. Never show the owner an image with an unexplained FAIL.
5. Keep checks light as `marketing-craft/references/image-review.md` §1 says (stage budgets: `image-pipeline.md` §1; video stills: `channel-kits.md` §3); report images made and sheets looked at against those budgets.
6. Every platform number you use has a source and a read date; re-read the live page when the fact-check date is older than your decision. Logged-in-only facts are "owner to confirm".
7. No proof stage (J024): the green-light studies (value, light, real-size `thumb_check.py`, the real layout) are self-checks inside your build run, measured again by the studio-operator's scripts.
8. Check what you received against the brief before you build on it. When a note carries a solution ("make it pop", "bigger logo"), restate the problem behind it, fix that, and re-check the problem, not only the change.
