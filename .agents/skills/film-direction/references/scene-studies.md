## Disagreements to keep (N3 SD)
- Close-detail fragmentation (Lyne) vs moving masters with internal compositions (EFAP): different scenes, neither licenses "always cut faster" or "always avoid cuts".
- Keep the line (continuity teaching) vs sacrifice it for emotion (Murch): a priority trade-off, not a contradiction.
- Boards matched one-to-one so animators can focus on acting (an animated series' first season) vs layout free to invent beats (Disney): **declare per sequence** whether boards are spatial and acting constraints or layout may propose alternatives (N3 BP-01). This is the `board_policy` field of the shot list.
- Authored camera (God of War) vs first-person events (Half-Life): different goals; Laidlaw's preference for linear narrative is his opinion.
