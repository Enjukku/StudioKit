direction and sourced examples; Table B gives first-draft numbers (all (P), from pack rows EM01-EM25), then A/B in
Studio. Mode cues hold for Western-trained listeners only (S-02, core). "st" = semitone.

## C. Emotion rules

| ID | Rule | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| S-26 | Aim for middle predictability and middle uncertainty (inverted U). | study |
| S-27 | Tension build: raise loudness, pitch height, onset rate, tempo and harmonic distance together; weights unknown. | inference (lead) |
| S-28 | Chill moment: hold one layer back 8-16 bars, enter a new voice with +3 to +6 dB, 2-4 s crescendo and one unexpected harmony change. Only a minority feel chills. (P) | study (correlational) + inference |
| S-29 | Before the chill lift, dip the mix −6 to −10 dB for about 2 s. (P) | inference |
| S-31 | Close, soft, detailed sound for menus, safe zones, idle and collecting (HR −1.9 bpm in all listeners); tingles are a bonus (20-41.7%). | study |
| S-32 | Personal-reward sounds close and dry: reverb tail <0.3 s, high-pass only at 30-60 Hz; wide space only for world events. (P) | study + inference |
| S-33 | Charge-ups and countdowns may end unresolved (5th, leading tone) and resolve on payoff only when a visible, deterministic timer or bar drives them. | practice |
| S-34 | Silence as a major cue at most once per area; a 30-40 s set-piece gap for one moment only. Test: players mention it unprompted. | practice |
| S-35 | Silence alone does not raise arousal; the contrast with the next event does. | inference (lead) |
| S-184 | Chill likelihood falls as chill moments repeat in a session (intensity +0.371 per exposure). Space them; keep them earned. | study (N=58) |
| S-185 | Test chills and tingles with two separate questions; report non-responders; keep liking, familiarity and arousal separate. | inference |
| S-187 | Gap before a noncritical reveal: A/B 0, 100, 250, 500, 1000 ms. Input feedback stays immediate. (P) | inference |
| S-188 | Nostalgia is built: a 2-4-note motif across 3-5 meaningful moments, re-orchestrated on return. Lo-fi filtering alone does not create memory. (P) | inference (lead) |
