## 1. Short videos and trailers
| Fact | Tag | Src |
| --- | --- | --- |
| YouTube lists, for video recommendations: whether viewers watch or skip, average view duration, average percentage viewed, likes, post-watch surveys; no mandatory format, no universal winning percentage (not confirmed as Shorts-specific) | V | FS-02..03 corrected |
| YouTube: publish time is not known to affect long-term performance (it matters for Live and Premiere); it recommends a consistent, sustainable schedule and gives no number. The paper's "no minimum Shorts cadence" sentence could not be re-read | V | FS-04..05; FS-01 unverified |
| Shorts can be up to 3 minutes. A maximum is not an optimum | V | FS-06 |
| YouTube public views: Shorts count every start or replay since 2025-03-31; from 2026-08-24 the first-frame view counts across all formats; engaged views are still reported; average view duration uses engaged views | V | FS-07, FS-09, FS-11 |
| TikTok paid-ad guidance (June 2025): proposition in the first 3 s, hook within 6 s, captions for context. Ad guidance, not an organic algorithm rule | V | FS-16 |
| "90 % of ad recall in the first 6 s" is a 2020 TikTok ad study, not a 2026 Roblox fact | V | FS-17 corrected |
| TikTok: use the Commercial Music Library for business content; other music licenses do not cover commercial use | V | FS-19 |
| Instagram Trial Reels go to non-followers first; optional auto-share after the first 72 h of views. A product feature, not a randomized test. Meta: 40 % of creators posted more after trying it, and 80 % **of those** saw more non-follower reach | V / E | FS-22..24 |
| Steam (contrast only): first trailer mainly gameplay from the player's view; check it with sound off | V | FS-28..29 |

**Practice (O):** treat the first 3 seconds as a review checkpoint: a legible player action and its surprising consequence before any lore; cut loading, walking and logo openings (SV-08..09). Use the shortest cut that makes the action clear and pays it off; compare a short payoff clip with a longer explaining cut instead of assuming 15/30/60 s (SV-10). Vertical edit keeps gameplay readable; captions explain the decision and stay off the app's buttons and the game's key UI; review once muted, once with sound (SV-11). Keep a clean gameplay master, a caption-free master and each platform edit; record music license, platforms, paid use, territory and expiry (SV-12). Cadence = what the capture and edit pipeline can sustain; times from your own audience data; no "best time" tables (SV-14..15). Hook candidates that suit Roblox: a clever reversal, a machine chain, a cooperative rescue, a genuinely rare transformation, a player build, each only if the build delivers it (SV-09).

## 2. Creators
| Fact | Tag | Src |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FTC (US): influencer and brand both carry the duty to disclose a material connection clearly; platform tools are no guarantee; in video disclose visually and audibly; brands train and monitor endorsers | V | FS-32..33 |
| YouTube: branded content needs the paid-promotion setting; the creator is responsible for a clear disclosure; branded-content videos are removed from YouTube Kids (a "kid-friendly creator" is no workaround) | V | FS-12..14 |
| YouTube 2026-09-03: age and country controls for declared paid promotion are live; automated detection is announced for "the coming months" (news coverage only) | V | FS-15 corrected |
| TikTok: commercial content disclosure setting required for own-business and third-party branded content; tagging a brand partner can grant ad authorization, which is separate from disclosure | V | FS-20..21 |
| Meta: partnership ads for eligible creator content | V | FS-26 |
| No public rate card, CPM, cost per player or conversion rate for Roblox creators exists in the sources | O (evidence gap) | CR-07 |

**Practice (O, CR-01..08):** buy an audience and a deliverable, not a follower count. The creator brief names the real promise, language, region, platform, the creator's recent relevant videos (median views on comparable recent videos, audience geography, never one best post), deliverables, dates, disclosure wording, allowed edits, usage and paid-amplification rights, approval limits, measurement access. Honest reactions welcome; paid praise never required. One share link per creator. Aggregate reports only, never children's personal data. A minor creator: an authorized adult and the owner's legal review before any agreement. Start with a bounded deliverable. Fees: a written quote per campaign, split into production, licensing, paid amplification, agency and any performance part. The owner approves, contacts and pays.

## 3. Roblox Ads Manager (V unless marked; read 2026-10-21)
| Fact | Src |
| --- | --- |
| Access 13+ with a verified email; payment cards 18+; Robux → ad credit from 13+, minimum 1 credit, **irreversible**; no fixed Robux-to-credit ratio documented (see the live purchase preview); the old "285 Robux" rule has no source | FA-01..03, FA-25..26 |
| Placement automatic on Home and Search; targeting by location, age, gender, genre, device, subject to audience eligibility | FA-08..09 |
| Audiences: All · New (never played, or 180+ days away) · Recent (last 30 days; minimum 10,000 players) · Lapsed (30-180 days; minimum 20,000 players) | FA-04..05 |
| Attribution windows (docs): first-time users 30 days; played < 7 days ago = the clicked session only; away 7-29 days = 7 days; away 30+ days = 30 days. The 2026-05-07 post changed assist credit and added New / Returning / Resurrected filters, without window lengths | FA-06..07 corrected |
| Creatives 16:9; count conflict: docs say up to 10, the 2026-05-07 staff post says 25 → owner checks the form. Docs say even distribution; the 2025 promise of engagement-based optimization is not confirmed live → treat delivery as unresolved | FA-10..12 |
| Fractional automated bidding, no manual bid; demand can delay serving; post-click data can take 48 h | FA-13..14 |
| Objectives: Plays; Earnings ("Limited", rolling out to eligible games, about 30,000 by 2026-10-02); Engagement (beta, for reaching highly engaged players toward Kids/Select eligibility). Eligibility shows in the interface | FA-15..18 corrected |
| After publish, payment method, goal, audience and budget type are fixed; campaigns can be cancelled up to 6 h before start | FA-61, FA-63 |
| Minimum spend: none on the docs page; the Open Cloud Ads API enforces an undisclosed minimum; the new featured tile (managed beta) needs a $5K USD campaign. No maximum account spend confirmed | FA-23..24 corrected |
| ROAS includes Creator Rewards, Immersive Ads and Rewarded Video earnings, shown in USD with rate differences; estimated ROAS is a forecast while earnings accrue | FA-19..21 |
| Ad campaigns for non-unique games may get no spend | FA-66 |
| DevEx: $0.0038 per eligible Earned Robux after 2025-09-05 10:00 PT; older balances $0.0035 (cashed out first); $0.0054 only for purchases of developer products, passes, subscriptions and private servers by U.S. players verified 18+ in eligible games, from 2026-06-08 | FA-27..29 |
| **E:** Roblox-reported randomized studies, 21 campaigns, all with incremental plays; median +14 % incremental play sessions, +10 % D7 retained users. Ink Game spotlight: $250/day, 277 % return. Not budgets, not forecasts, no cost accounting to infer profit | FA-58..59, FS-55..56 |

**Practice (O, AD-12..20):** Plays for a bounded acquisition test; Earnings only when the goal is monetization and the game is eligible; Engagement only for its own purpose. An ad test has a total loss ceiling, a duration, a funding source, auto-reload off, a pause owner and an analyst spec. Two ledgers: platform-attributed ROAS, and the studio's incremental contribution (incremental net receipts minus ads, creator, asset and operating costs). Never compare gross player Robux spend with a USD media bill; never convert all receipts at the U.S. 18+ rate. Ad-arrived players never improve RFY ranking (FD-10). A holdout is the only route to "incremental"; otherwise say "attributed".

## 4. Updates, events, notifications (V, read 2026-10-21)
| Fact | Src |
| --- | --- |
| Events (Creator Hub → Engagement → Events & Updates): up to 10 ongoing or upcoming | FD-53 |
| Trending Events: a public active event, started within 7 days, at least 1,000 RSVPs; the sort uses game DAU. Eligibility, not guaranteed reach | FD-54 |
| Update announcements: 60 characters, at most once per 3 days, to opted-in players; stats: views, visit rate, unfollow rate | FD-55..56 |
| Off-platform featuring: docs say submit at least 7 days ahead; the 2025-12-10 launch post said 14. Not automatic; eligible events are reviewed, no feedback if not chosen, featured at no cost. Pilot art spec 1920×1080 or 1080×1920, or a 4000×4000 layered PSD (PILOT, 2025-12-10) | FD-57..60 corrected |
| Personalized experience notifications: opted-in users 13+; one per user per day per experience; delivery not guaranteed; dashboard needs 100 impressions (summary) and 100 per notification (itemized); CTR = clicks ÷ impressions; a changed string makes a new row: one string per variant; under-13 opted-in players get update notices, not personalized notifications | FD-61..64 |

**Practice (O, UP-10..11):** every message points to a real, live reason to return; a limit is a ceiling, not a cadence. Record event id, build availability, time zone, assets, approval, audience, destination and measurement window; check the event's spawn path before promoting it. Count reach from impressions, never from everyone opted in. Plan featuring with a 14-day studio buffer, never promise featuring. No urgent or pushy wording to minors (FA-51).

## 5. Community (Discord)
| Fact | Tag | Src |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discord minimum age 13, higher where local law requires ("up to 16" not re-confirmed) | V | FS-36..37 |
| 2026-09-22: users grouped adult / teen / unconfirmed; teen protections (non-friend DMs go to requests, adult content limits); the age group is hidden from other members | V | FS-38 |
| AutoMod coverage (all servers, English-only lists, multi-language custom keywords): not confirmed (FAQ blocked); a moderation page says setup needs Communities enabled | unverified | FS-39..41 |
| Discord Social SDK integrations for Roblox games (2026-05-28): account-linked access, Rich Presence, invites, deauthorize anytime. Roblox's 2026-06-02 notice still bans cross-platform tracking and sending user data out: review every data flow first | V | FS-42, FA-55..57 |
| E: Medal clips created/shared (Untitled Boxing Game 147K/28K, War Tycoon 319K/181K, BloxStrike 449K/213K), no window, spend or retention; Sol's RNG figures are estimates | E | FS-53..54 |

**Practice (O, CM-04..05):** few posting channels, clear rules, a named human for escalation, AutoMod tested in each community language, anti-scam guidance, an incident log. Essentials (support, patch notes, rewards) also in game; Discord never required. Feedback forms ask for bug and category, never birthdays, IDs, schools or contacts. Staff never seek private chats with minors. Clips and shares are activity, not acquisition, until arrivals and player quality are measured.
