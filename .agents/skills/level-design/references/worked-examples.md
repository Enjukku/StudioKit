# Worked examples

## 1. Review: "players miss the shop" in a fishing-simulator harbour hub
- **Job card first.** Target experience: collection and comfort. Loop: fish at the pier end, sell at the market, buy a rod at the shop, reach the next sea's gate. Session: return visits of about 40 s per sell loop on phones. Walk speed 16 studs/s; jump not yet measured (starting value 6.4, probe requested).
- **Cause before fix.** The note says "add a big arrow to the shop". The problem behind it: new players walk spawn → pier → market → pier and never pass the shop. The route JSON shows the shop is 30 studs off the sell loop behind the market hall; in the first-view capture (C2) the ranking is fountain, market, gate; the shop is not in frame. The cue is off-camera on approach, not too small.
- **Route table.** Market → pier end 243 studs = 15.2 s, over the brief's 10 s limit with nothing on the way: the sell loop itself is too long (C8, LD4).
- **Findings, ranked.** 1 C2 Fail: the spawn faces the fountain; turning the spawn 35° frames the gate with the shop sign in front of it (small). 2 C8 Fail: the sell loop is 15 s each way; a second sell stall at the pier's midpoint cuts it to 8 s (medium; a game-designer decision because it changes the loop's timing). 3 C3 Fail: the shop sits off the loop; moving its door to face the market path puts it on the way (medium). Keep: the lighthouse is the tallest shape and differs from every other landmark (IoU 0.31 against the market hall).
- **Not done:** no arrow, no glowing shop. The fountain was calmed one value step instead of brightening the shop (contrast before detail). Could not check: C13 (no two-client server), C19 (the quality-level probe has not run).

## 2. Plan and blockout: a lava obby world that teaches the moving platform
- **Job card.** Target experience: mastery of a challenge. Teaches: timing a moving platform. Session: a short phone session of 5-8 minutes; checkpoints every stage.
- **Layout options.** A: a straight climb up the volcano (warm-up). B: a spiral round the cone, the crater always in view. C (atypical): a collapsed mine railway, the platforms as mine carts on rails. Pick B: the crater stays the landmark from every point, so C9 progress is visible all the way; C's carts are kept as the beat-3 twist. A lost: no landmark change, no view to pause on.
- **Teaching sheet.** Beat 1: one slow platform over a floor of plain rock (falling costs 3 s). Beat 2: the same platform over lava, checkpoint just before. Beat 3: two platforms moving out of phase (the cart idea). Beat 4: a platform plus the conveyor taught in the previous world, no hint.
- **Pacing map** (the whole spiral, 81 s computed). Rest 6 s, build 9 s, peak 4 s (15-19 s), rest with a view of the crater 7 s, build 8 s, peak 6 s (34-40 s), rest 10 s, build 17 s, climax 14 s at the crater rim (67-81 s). Gaps between peaks, end to start: 15 s and 27 s (uneven); the climax starts at 67 s, in the last third (after 54 s); the vista of the next world opens on the rim, a real achievement.
- **Blockout.** Spawn, crater goal and the spiral first; grey Parts on a 1-stud grid; a dummy and a door-sized frame on every ledge. The walk test timed the spiral at 94 s against 81 s computed (16 % over: a narrow ledge slows the walk; within tolerance). Ledges: the lip of beat 2's landing at 6.0 studs is meant to be climbed and sits under the starting jump by less than the margin: CHECK, lowered to 4.5 before the art pass.
- **Grey read pass.** First view: the crater rim ranks first by median and its own contrast against the sky, 4.2, is above the marker target; one fork view showed a dead end of 40 × 30 studs with no use: it gained a secret coin and a rest bench.
