# Diagnosis: symptom → cause → proof → fix

**Rule:** find the cause and prove it before changing anything; fix at the least disruptive layer; retest everything the fix touches; say what stays unverified. A builder's checks passing never overrules a screenshot (FS-V05).

| Symptom | Likely causes (check all) | Proof before any change | Fix | Do not |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **"It looks flat; I don't know where to look"** | no deliberate hierarchy, not too little light; no focal point; competing surfaces as bright as the goal | compare the focal region with the surfaces competing with it, at the player camera, low and high quality | change the light / material relationship between focal region and competitors; if there is no focal point, a bolder large form or a clearer sightline, not a brighter small prop (DF01 O) | add lights everywhere; brighten the whole scene |
| **Clutter: busy but meaningless; players bump into props** | every free surface treated as a place for props; no exclusion zones for walk lanes, interaction approaches and camera corridors | overlay the route lanes and attention exclusions on the dressing; list low-value clusters | re-apply the exclusions and clear the lanes; remove low-value clusters; where emptiness is wrong, add one meaningful structural or story relationship, not scatter (DF02 O; PR-17, PR-21) | turn collision off on props players can reach to hide the problem |
| **Dollhouse scale, hovering props, obvious repeats, weak terrain joins** | an unverified conversion somewhere in the scale chain, or assets made without the player reference; contact not validated; repetition judged only on stills | measure the calibration object and the player dummy; trace the chain in order: scene units, object transforms, export conversion, importer scaling (`environment-craft/references/blender-to-studio.md` §2) | fix the source of the mismatch before resizing single objects; then contact (audit floating list), then repetition with a moving camera, then terrain joins from both sides with collision view (DF03 O) | rescale descendants one by one; stack scale factors until it "looks right" |
| **Zones sound the same; sound jumps at a border; a sound heard where its cause is not** | no ambience map, or one global loop; a hard switch at a border; streaming restarts or doubles a source; a point source placed as a non-positional bed or with no end distance; a duplicate in the wrong place | the ambience map vs the scene audit (ids, positions, kinds); walk the border both ways; leave → return → leave; check each source's anchor, position mode and curve end | per-zone causal layers and blends with the sound designer; streaming-safe homes and rebuild-once rules; positional sources at their visible cause with an end distance set by the sound designer (DF04 O; `ambience.md`) | treat it as "a bad asset" before checking state, parenting and duplicates |
| **The stage runs badly on a phone** | cost never traced to resources; instance count alone cannot tell a marker from an expensive system | phone trace of the busiest overlap with gameplay on; Scene Analysis and MicroProfiler to find CPU / GPU / physics / texture / streaming pressure (`review.md` §5) | reduce the measured bottleneck one category at a time while keeping the scene's essential information; ask the owner what to protect when cuts are needed (FS-P11) | delete props by count; pass the budget by turning ambience or effects off (a stage that passes only that way has not passed) (DF05 O) |
| **Ground jumps up and down in chunks round buildings; grass above a floor; a landform sunk into the ground** (owner does not want a flat map) | building floor heights set independently of the ground round them; decorative relief or noise adding unintended steps; landforms on a different base height | measure the height step between neighbouring ground samples and compare floor heights with the ground at each building edge; sort steps into designed relief and unintended artifacts | smooth blend, not a flat map: fixed anchors (walkways, floors and their rings, water), limited step between neighbours, intended relief kept; adjust floor heights where the ring cannot blend; re-measure after (FS hidden case HC1; `environment-craft/references/terrain-and-water.md` §3) | flatten the whole map; raise every building |
| **A review render shows wrong colours (white looks grey, pastel looks olive) while the palette values match** | the preview or Blender render set-up (ambient light, exposure, colour management) shifts colours; the model is fine | render a white / grey / palette test card in the same set-up; compare with a Studio capture under the game's real lighting | fix and record the review set-up; judge final colour in Studio; change the palette only if the Studio capture also differs (FS hidden case HC2) | edit model colours or start a colour polish round from preview renders |
| **Every check passes, but a screenshot shows gaps (slats, lines) in a roof or wall** | missing faces between stepped layers (the vertical risers), or a real gap between pieces; or a render artifact; or an authored detail (a chimney notch) | re-shoot from the angle where it shows plus a low oblique and an under-roof view, at two quality levels; inspect the source geometry: a gap that exists in the data is real, a line that moves with the camera or vanishes at another quality is an artifact; check each finding against the blueprint before calling it a defect | add the missing faces without changing footprint or heights; leave authored details alone; report what is data-verified and what still needs a fresh capture (FS hidden case HC3) | trust an outline score over the picture; lower a threshold; rebuild the whole roof |
