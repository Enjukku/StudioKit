Purpose: a craft reference for lighting, haze, sky, cloud, post-effect, local-light, material and water choices in Roblox, built only from the docs. Use it to make deliberate choices, then confirm every choice with Studio screenshots (open questions in section 9 feed the G2 look lab). Facts already settled in [[reference/environment/fact-check-lighting]] keep their ids (LF/LX) and are not re-checked.

Rules for reading: every fact has `file:line`. "example: <scene>" = one scene's tutorial value, never a preset. "inference" = my reasoning from cited facts, not a doc statement. Paraphrased; no quote over 15 words. No fact comes from memory.
