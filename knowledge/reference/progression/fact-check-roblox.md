## Answers to the two open questions (check C4)

**Rounding-display treatment (Z08, FB07b): resolved.** The paper never states it. `PM/paid-random-items.md:55` (identical upstream, live, DF 4654622 FAQ): odds are shown as percentages that sum to exactly 100 %. For long decimals the developer may round to four or more decimal places below the place of the first non-zero digit, and must add a disclaimer. The page's example text: "Probabilities are rounded; total of displayed individual probabilities may not equal 100%." (12 words). My reading of "four or more places lower": for 0.0123456789 % the first non-zero digit sits in the 2nd decimal place, so keep at least the 6th decimal place (0.012346 %). That is at least 5 significant digits. The page gives no worked example, so this is my reading; showing more digits is always safe. No rounding is allowed without the disclaimer.

**5 versus 10 resource types (DI7, FB36): unresolved by the docs; marked unverified.** Today the three pages say: `PA/event-types.md:99-102` table row "Resource types ... 10" (Economy only); `PA/economy-events.md:121` "You can add up to five currencies of resources" (Economy dashboard); `Y/AnalyticsService.yaml:474` "Limited to 5 unique currency types per experience" (parameter `currencyType` of `LogEconomyEvent`). All three are identical at upstream main (2026-10-20 20:05 UTC). I found no Roblox post that settles it. A 2025-08 DevForum thread (3890573) repeats the 10 from the docs; a reply relayed there says stop logging old currencies and they drop out next day (limits are daily). Only a published game sending events can test it. Rule for the skill: plan for 5 or fewer currencies, treat 10 as unconfirmed, and read the Economy dashboard after publishing.
