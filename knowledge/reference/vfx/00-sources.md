## Tiers and evidence levels

| tier | meaning (per-game research brief, not shipped) | use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | First-hand work by the makers (GDC/SIGGRAPH talks, official studio blogs, patch notes, maker writing, first-party code) and peer-reviewed studies. Preprints are A and tagged "(preprint)". | Can carry a rule alone, within its scope. |
| spec | Engine, platform and standards documentation: Roblox Creator Docs (local mirror `knowledge/external/roblox-creator-docs/`), Unity/Unreal docs, W3C, ITU, EBU, store policy. | What a system can do or requires. Never proof that something feels good. |
| B | Trade press that interviews makers (VFX Voice, befores & afters, 80 Level, Game Developer, fxguide, Art of VFX...). | Good support; quotes are relayed. |
| C | Expert community and teachers: realtimevfx threads, VFX Apprentice, DevForum posts by known creators, vendor blogs, named practitioners. | A claim resting only on C needs a second source. |
| D | General blogs, fan wikis, news without maker access, search snippets, search tools. | Lead only. Never cite as proof. |
| internal | This project's own earlier notes. | Context, not external evidence. |

- `+` / `-` keep the input's nuance (C+ = named maker on a forum; B- = blogger or relayed trade press; A- = first-party code or a small study).
- Tier is provenance only. Evidence level (study, preprint, industry practice, spec, inference) is tagged per claim in the reference files, and a tier-A source can still be off-scope for Roblox (GPT README: engine caps and tutorial values are not budgets).
- Read scope is the best scope reached by either run: full > abstract > description only (session/course/listing page) > second-hand (search excerpt, indexed copy) > unreadable. A row read only at "abstract" or "description only" supports metadata and stated scope, not method details.
- Year is the publication or page date; "live" = living docs page; "n.d." = no date found. pre-2020 is computed from the year.
- Aliases: `claude:<file>#<id>` and `gpt:<id>`. "(part)" marks a Claude row that bundled several docs pages or talks; in `source-aliases.json` such an alias maps to its first page (see Composite aliases).
