# VFX reference 04: field methods (Part 4) and flipbook and parts rules (Part 8)

Scope and use: how makers in 8 fields make an effect work, turned into instructions for our Roblox brick-destruction game (section 1), and the rules for picking, kitbashing, timing, blending and sizing flipbooks and other Roblox VFX parts (section 3). Merged from the Claude research files (p4-*, gap-film-anime-flipbook, gap-destruction-feel, gap-mobile-roblox-games) and the ChatGPT package (04_field_methods_table, supporting_reference kitbashing, claim_ledger), with Roblox facts checked in the local docs mirror.
Keys: sources are R-ids from `00-sources.md` with register tier and date; "pubs N" = distinct publications behind the row (both models citing one publication counts once). Every number carries an R-id and an evidence level. "PROPOSAL (inference)" values are starting points with no direct evidence; all design numbers stay proposals until the owner confirms them.
