# VFX reference 07: Roblox table (Part 11), performance and mobile (Part 10), accessibility and safety (Part 9)

**Scope and use.** Engine features a VFX designer agent may combine on Roblox, what they cost on phones, and the safety rules every effect must pass. Read the Roblox table before choosing parts and properties; check every effect card against the Part 10 budgets and the Part 9 rules before review.
Every Roblox property name, range and limit below was re-read in the local docs mirror on 2026-10-12; where the mirror and either research input differ, the mirror wins and the row is listed under Conflicts.
`PROPOSAL (inference)` values are starting points with a basis and a test, numbered [P01]..[P26]. They are not owner-approved (owner rule: design numbers are proposals until confirmed).

Legend. `D/` = `knowledge/external/roblox-creator-docs/`; `A/` = `D/_api-reference/` (`classes/`, `enums/`, `datatypes/`); `:N` = line in that file. Sources are unified ids from `00-sources.md`; former `UNREG:` ids now resolve to R-450..R-467 (mapping at the end). Evidence level per number: study / preprint / industry practice (who, year) / spec / inference. "single tier C" = only one tier-C publication supports it. Distinct publications are counted, not model agreement.

| General flash (definition) | A pair of opposing relative-luminance changes of at least 10% of maximum relative luminance (1.0), with the darker state below 0.80; one up-then-down pair = one flash. | WCAG 2.2 SC 2.3.1 (AA) pass routes | Content passes if EITHER no more than 3 general and no more than 3 red flashes in any 1 s, OR the combined area of concurrent flashes is at most 0.006 sr within any 10-degree visual field (= 25% of that field) at typical viewing distance.
