## Use in skills

Cite each line with its RC id and page. Confirmed rows only; there are no corrected rows.

- RC01: FY2025 average DAUs split mobile 83%, desktop 14%, console 3% (10-K FY2025, printed p. 9). An annual average, not a session split.
- RC02: Platform average 2.7 hours per DAU per day in 2025 (10-K FY2025, p. 9). Not a per-game session.
- RC04, RC05: Q2 2026 DAUs about 123 million and hours about 29 billion (Q2 2026 letter; data sheet p. 3). Q1 2026 DAUs were 132 million.
- RC06, RC07: 57% of DAUs age-checked in the last week of June 2026; of those, 35% under 13, 38% age 13-17, 27% age 18+. Age-checked subset only.
- RC08: Q2 2026 DAUs by region (millions): US and Canada 22, Europe 26, Asia-Pacific 41, rest of world 34.
- RC09, RC10: Average session time = total time / sessions. Roblox advises fun within the first five minutes and no long tutorials (engagement page).
- RC11: D1, D7, D30 by first-play cohort; D7 is the 8th day; recent cohort cells can be empty; Roblox gives no good thresholds (retention page).
- RC12-RC15: Benchmarks update daily, show the 50th-90th percentile of similar games with at least 100 DAU, fall back to genre then all experiences; overall-success tiers use the top 1000 games by rolling 30-day playtime, games over 30 days old only (analytics dashboard page). The 12.11%-18.73% D1 band is the page's example only.
- RC16-RC18: Recommended For You looks across 28 days (day 1, days 2-7, days 8-28); new games get distribution at once; the playtime signal caps at 60 minutes per user per game per day; benchmarks have no effect on ranking (R07, discovery, discovery FAQ).

## Changed since the paper

No page I read states a rule that contradicts the paper. The current docs add the following, which the paper left out or kept soft:

- Paid random odds rounding: the paper said only "a rounding exception". The docs now give the rule (RC22).
- Benchmarks: the paper says "not direct ranking inputs". The docs say they have no impact on the algorithm in any way (RC18).
- Paid random items, Community Standards: every outcome must give the buyer some benefit, so no outcome may simply lose the payment. Random rewards earned without Robux or paid currency need no odds (RC21).
- Discovery: a custom thumbnail is required to get Home recommendation impressions; leading with giveaways or Robux claims, mismatched metadata and non-unique games all limit exposure (discovery page).
- Discovery: play-through and first-play bounce measure only a user's first session from Home recommendations; later returns count only in the day 2-7 and day 8-28 signals (FAQ).
- Advertising Standards: the page on-screen date is 2026-10-09 but the Help Center metadata says 2026-10-12. The October 9 update loosened some Portal Ads limits. Details not read; recheck before launch (RC24, RC26).
- Rewarded video: reward may not be a random item; publisher needs about 2,000 unique monthly visitors and ID verification (mirror rewarded-video-ads page).
- Ad integrations: campaigns under review are shown only to users 18 and older (ad integrations page).
- Kids and Select: expedited review costs 50,000 Robux (refundable); "highly engaged" includes platform spend in the last 60 days (RC30).
- Q2 2026 DAUs (123 million) are below Q1 2026 (132 million); Roblox's own R07 article uses 132 million (RC04).
- Roblox sources disagree on the month of the Grow a Garden peak (RC43).
