How I checked. Read date for every row is 2026-10-21 (today). I read the studio's own page where it was reachable, and I read the raw page text, not only a summary, for every row marked "confirmed" or "corrected", except SF10 (LinkedIn), where only a WebFetch summary was available. Tools used:
- WebFetch and WebSearch for first contact.
- `curl` from the shell for raw HTML, the Steam news JSON feed (`api.steampowered.com/ISteamNews/GetNewsForApp`, app 1422450), and PDFs (`pdftotext`). I used it when WebFetch returned an empty summary (Steam, Riot, PDF pages).
- The built-in browser pane for pages that draw their text with JavaScript (playdeadlock.com, the Dota 2 Workshop guide, Epic's tech blog).
- The Internet Archive (Wayback Machine) for pages that no longer load: Riot's retired Nexus site (now redirects to the League home page) and the MobaFire reprint (HTTP 403 to bots). A Wayback copy of the paper's own URL is the same document; I say so in the row. A copy of a third-party reprint stays third-party.
- A Steam page has no body text for a web fetch (it only returns the title). The same posts are in the Steam news JSON feed, which I used. The feed gives the post date and the full text.

Verdicts (from the ticket):
- confirmed: the source says what the notes say, within the scope given.
- corrected: the source says something different or narrower; the row gives the correct statement.
- unverified: source unreachable or silent; keep the claim as EXPERIENCE or OPINION.
- dropped: the source contradicts the claim, or the claim is not in the source and was presented as the studio's.

Provenance labels:
- studio-published: the studio published it as a product page, announcement, official guide or paper.
- developer or artist account: a person at the studio tells how they worked (blog, interview, Q&A). It is an account of one project, not a studio rule.
- third-party: a fan wiki, forum, reprint or press outlet.
- paper's reading: the paper's own interpretation. It is never a studio statement.

A quote is a locating excerpt of 15 words or fewer. Everything else is my paraphrase with the place named.

Coverage (details in "Coverage and skips" at the end): all priority 1 claims (every Deadlock and League claim) checked; all priority 2 numbers on studio sources checked; all priority 3 lesson-bearing claims checked; priority 4 (the rest) checked except R20, R19-as-claim and the standards.
