## How I checked

- Date of every read: 2026-10-21 (the date in the `checked against` column). I read live pages with WebFetch and WebSearch. No verdict comes from memory.
- WebFetch returns a model extract of the page, not the raw page. Every "source says" cell is therefore extract text or a paraphrase of it. Quotes are 15 words or fewer.
- Where the cited page could not be rendered, I looked for a second official page of the same publisher. The row says so in `note`. If only news coverage existed, the row says "secondary only".
- Case numbers: I checked the figure, the reporter, the unit, the window and that the cited page holds it. Every case result stays EXPERIENCE with its scope. None is a rule, benchmark or forecast.
- Verdict words: confirmed / corrected (correct statement given) / unverified (not found or not readable; keep as labelled OPINION or drop) / dropped (none needed).
- Legal rows (FS-32 to FS-35) confirm only what the official pages say. They are not legal advice.
- I did not look at any page outside the notes' own source list, except the substitute official pages named in the rows.
