Scope: every Roblox claim in the rigger paper (RP, `Roblox_Character_Rigger_Research.txt`) and the animator paper (AP, `Animator_Research_Paper.txt`) that a rig or animation skill could turn into a rule. Blender, Tripo and AI-motion-tool claims are out of scope (J005-T07, J005-T08).

How I checked. Mirror = `knowledge/external/roblox-creator-docs/`, revision 7400643, refreshed 2026-10-20 (`README.md:7`). I read it on 2026-10-21. Where the mirror lacks a page or the claim is a dated forum post, I read the live page on 2026-10-21. Forum posts are not release notes; I label them. Paths below are relative to the mirror root unless they start with `knowledge/`. `ref/` = `_api-reference/classes/`.

Verdicts: confirmed (source matches the paper), corrected (source gives a different value), partly (matches with a limit or a missing piece), unverified (not found; I say what I searched), contradicted (source says the opposite).

## Rules the skills may state

Each line is a short rule with its citation. "Mirror" means the repo-relative path above. Wording is mine.

1. A general mesh may have at most 20,000 triangles. Avatar bodies use their own budgets. (RR01; `art/modeling/specifications.md:34`)
2. Avatar body budgets: head 4000, torso 1750, each limb asset 1248, total 10,742 triangles. Count tris, not quads. (RR02; `avatar/character-bodies/specifications.md:381-431`)
3. A vertex may have at most 4 bone influences. No weight on the Root bone. (RR03, RR04; `art/modeling/specifications.md:59-60`)
4. Geometry must be watertight, in quads where possible, with real thickness. (RR05; `art/modeling/specifications.md:35-37`)
5. Freeze transforms: scale 1, rotation 0, Root at the origin. (RR30; `art/modeling/specifications.md:56-58`)
6. An avatar body is 15 named `_Geo` meshes, facing +Z, standing in +Y, in I, A or T pose. Advanced R15 adds up to 37 optional joints, each with its prescribed name and parent. (RR06, RR07, RR20)
7. After importing an advanced R15 body, add HumanoidRigDescription and DigitsRigDescription objects. (RR08)
8. Bone animation moves the picture, not the collision shape. Give physics its own proxy parts. (RR12; `art/modeling/rigging.md:49`)
9. Use `Animator:LoadAnimation`. The AnimationController version is deprecated. (RR13)
10. Dynamic heads: 50 base FACS poses exist; 17 are required to sell; neutral on frame 0; one pose per frame after that. Keep face joints under 50 where possible. (RR15, RR16, RR17, RR34)
11. Blender FBX export for general models: Apply Scalings = FBX Unit Scale, Add Leaf Bones off, Bake Animation off unless the file carries animation. (RR18; `art/modeling/export-requirements.md:34-42`)
12. Blender FBX export for avatar bodies: Custom Properties on; NLA Strips, All Actions and Force Start/End Keyframes off; Simplify 0.0. (RR19; `avatar/character-bodies/export.md:62-72`)
13. Importer Rig Type is R15, Custom or No Rig. Keep Zero Influence Bones is off by default, so turn it on to keep sockets and a weightless Root. (RR21, RR22; `studio/importer.md:238, 269`)
14. glTF or GLB imports nest each mesh in its own Model with one MeshPart per primitive; FBX does not. Read the live importer page, section "glTF import hierarchy", as of 2026-10-21. (RR23)
15. Multi-clip FBX or glTF animation import is GA in the Animation Clip Editor (update of 2026-03-31, forum announcement). The general spec page still says one animation track per model export. (RR37, RR38)
16. Do not rely on root motion. A forum reply of 2026-01-21 says it was unreleased; the RDC26 roadmap lists it for Early 2027. Default locomotion to in-place clips. (RR39, RR41)
17. Animation Graphs are released (2026-07-15). State machines and Luau expressions are a Studio-only beta (2026-10-08). Do not ship content that needs the beta features. (RR42, RR43)
18. Animation priority, high to low: Action4, Action3, Action2, Action, Movement, Idle, Core. Evaluated per joint until weight reaches 1. Never compare priorities by enum number. (RR44; `_api-reference/enums/AnimationPriority.yaml:23-29`)
19. Load a track once and reuse it (`GetTrackByAnimationId`). The Animator must be in Workspace before `LoadAnimation`. (RR45)
20. Do not use `IsPlaying` to test active influence. `GetPlayingAnimationTracks` includes fading tracks. (RR46)
21. For NPCs: an Animator not inside a player's Character must load and start on the server to replicate; a client-created Animator's tracks never replicate. For many NPCs, Roblox suggests a client-side Animator for cosmetic motion. (RR47, RR58)
22. Use `GetMarkerReachedSignal` for event markers. `KeyframeReached` is deprecated. (RR54)
23. `Stop` starts a fade-out. Use `Ended` to know the track has no influence left. Avoid `Stop(0)` on Motor6Ds with MaxVelocity 0. (RR55, RR56)
24. Turn an IKControl off with `Enabled = false`. Weight 0 is not off. (RR57)
25. On R15 characters with Avatar Joint Upgrade on, joints are AnimationConstraints. Layer procedural motion into `Transform` in PreSimulation and skip it when `Animator.EvaluationThrottled` is true. Motor6D stays right for mechanical rigs. (RR10, RR48, RR51, RR52)
26. The Animator normally overwrites `Motor6D.Transform` each frame. Write custom offsets after the Animator and before physics. (RR51)
27. Animation Editor defaults to 30 fps (the rate can be changed). A loop does not blend from last to first key, so duplicate the first pose at the end. (RR64, RR65)
28. Marketplace animation packs: standard R15, original, CurveAnimation, 10 s or less per clip, loop except Jump. The 10 s limit is for packs only. (RR63)
29. Video body capture needs a single continuous shot under 15 s and an R15 rig. Face capture records up to 60 s and is a beta feature. (RR35, RR36)
30. Profile on a phone. Whole-frame budgets are 33.33 ms at 30 FPS and 16.67 ms at 60 FPS. (RR59)
31. `PreloadAsync` yields, reports a status per asset, and does not throw on a failed asset. Preload only what is needed soon. (RR60)

## Extra findings

- The mirror lags the live importer page: the glTF hierarchy section (RR23) exists only on the live site. A skill that cites the mirror alone will miss it. Re-sync the mirror (`python tools/sync_roblox_docs.py`) before the skills ship, then re-check whether the section arrives.
- The mirror's animation-graph pages for state machines and expressions carry no beta label, while the forum says Studio-only beta (RR43). Do not use the mirror as the release-status source for these.
- The body spec page says "Rig Type > Rthro" for the Normal scale (`avatar/character-bodies/specifications.md:68`), while the importer page lists Rthro under Rig Scale (`studio/importer.md:277-278`). The importer page is the better source for the label.
- `Animator.yaml:71-73` names a Workspace setting `ClientAnimatorThrottlingMode`; the actual property is `Workspace.ClientAnimatorThrottling` (`ref/Workspace.yaml:196`). A script that copies the first name will fail.
- `avatar-setup/auto-setup-requirements.md:157` limits body rigs to the R15 or advanced R15 armature and says facial rigs have no bone limit. This is for the Avatar Setup route, not for custom rigs.
- Not checked: RP R6 Creature tentacle detail beyond RR28; AP sample code in `ContentProvider` and `AnimationTrack` pages; Blender, Tripo, Mixamo and AI-motion claims (other tickets); Valve's 52-bone figure (non-Roblox).
