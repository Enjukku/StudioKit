| Measure readability from the shot cameras, not by eye | the 4:3-centre and screen-height check found the C beacon cut at 4:3 and the E avatar merged with the guardian; both fixed by moving cameras or the exit | keep |

| Regenerate a failed panel as an edit, one change | 3 of 3 edits fixed exactly the fault (touch, beacon state) | keep |
