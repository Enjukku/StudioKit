## Will an agent with these books make better work?
Yes. With the old books the qa-lead tested rules and engine behaviour only; nothing made it test that what players see tells the truth. With the new books, a sell button or a slam ability gets a rejected-request row with the server log as the oracle, a danger edge checked against the data radius, a burst at the real rate, reduced motion, grayscale and the busiest moment on a phone, and the hooks for all of these are asked for before the build. Each result says what its input can prove. The weak spots are small and fixable in one round: stale hook names (F1), a wrong jump default (F2), a finding field that differs from the master and the partner reviewer (F3), a missing pulse cap (F4), a worked example that breaks the image budget (F5) and a copied scan (F6). The book is long (SKILL.md plus a 21 KB catalogue), but the catalogue is in `references/` and each row is a test, not advice.
