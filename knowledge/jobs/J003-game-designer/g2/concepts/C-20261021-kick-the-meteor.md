| 6 | Core loop in one sentence | To reach a higher sky zone, I pick a meteor I can handle and time my kick (alone or with a friend's relay kick), then I see how high it flies and catch the loot that rains down, then the coins my loot earns buy kick power so I can take bigger meteors. |
| 7 | Signature moment | Setup: a shower warning; a house-sized meteor falls toward a group of friends. Decision: kick it alone (keep all the loot, lower zone) or relay (share, higher zone). Payoff: the moon cracks and moon rocks rain over the whole town; everyone runs to catch them. Normal inputs: one kick button with a timing bar, jump. Needs: meteor trail and impact VFX, a clear height read (zone bands in the sky), kick animation with weight, a camera that follows the meteor up and returns, a loud impact sound |

| 9 | Familiar foundation and original contribution | Borrowed (function only): kick for distance, the landing zone sets reward rarity, rewards earn at your plot, coins buy kick power (Kick a Lucky Block, DG2 description). Changed rule: the kick goes up, not along, and height picks the zone (clouds, orbit, moon, planets; names TBD); bigger meteors pay more but crash on your plot if your kick is too weak (a short repair, no item loss); friends can relay-kick one meteor in mid-air for a higher zone and a shared reward. Own expression: meteors, space loot, town, UI and sound are original; no lucky blocks, no "brainrot" collectibles, no tsunami chase, no weights shop look. Note: a space theme sits near the owner's own "Destroy the Sun" example; the finale avoids the sun |
| 10 | Smallest buildable version | Keeps the decisive action: kick a falling meteor and see how high it goes. Content: one hill town with one plot per player, meteors of 3 sizes on a timer, a one-button kick with a timing bar, 4 sky zones with a fixed loot table each (shown on screen), a plot shelf that pays coins, one upgrade line (kick power), a relay ring for 2 or more players. Excluded: more zones, rebirth, pets, trading, events, a finale. Dependencies: a scripted meteor arc (not a physics simulation; architect confirms), meteor and impact VFX (vfx-designer), kick animation (animator), timing bar and zone read (ui-ux-designer). Target devices: phone first (RC01); one-thumb input |
| 11 | Failure hypothesis | Strongest reason it may fail: after the first zones the loop is only bigger numbers meeting bigger meteors (`game-design` G-04), and players leave once they have seen the moon hit. Second: players read it as a reskin of its reference. Cheapest test: a grey-box (hill, meteor, timing-bar kick, 4 height bands, relay ring, one upgrade) with 4-6 target-age players for one session. Watch: do they choose relay over solo, and when do they stop kicking without a new zone in sight? Plus a title test: show the title only to 5 target players and ask "what will you do?" Result that changes the plan: no relay use and drop-off right after the moon → the twist is not enough |
