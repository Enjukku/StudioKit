# Pitch record: Guard the Dragon Egg

| 3 | One-line fantasy | As a keeper, I guard a giant dragon egg on a storm-swept mountain through the nights so that it hatches into our own dragon, while sky thieves try to carry it away. |
| 4 | Hook | The camp you defend is alive. The egg glows warmer when you feed it and cracks a little more each night. What the group feeds it (fire crystals, storm shards, frost berries) decides what hatches, and the dragon that hatches stays yours |
| 5 | First image | (concept art not made: no images in this task.) Night on a mountain ledge above the clouds. A giant glowing egg sits in a ring of torches. Three small players stand around it with lanterns and spears. Winged shapes circle in the lightning. Readable action: guarding. Scale: egg vs players. Stake: the shapes |
| 6 | Core loop in one sentence | To get the egg through the next night, we gather by day and choose what to feed the egg and what to build, then the night's thieves test the nest and the egg warms or chills, then we use what we earned to make the nest stronger and push toward the hatch night. |
| 7 | Signature moment | Setup: the last night, a storm, the egg nearly cracked. Decision: thieves lift the egg off the nest; one player throws the last fire crystal into it instead of at the thieves. Consequence: the egg cracks open in mid-air, and a baby dragon breathes its first fire; the thieves drop it. Payoff: the hatchling lands, looks at its keepers and follows them to their roost. Normal inputs: gather, throw, build, hit. Needs: egg states that read from far away (glow, cracks), a hatch animation, first-fire VFX, thief wind-up that reads at night on a phone, a storm-to-dawn light change, a strong hatch sound |
| 8 | Why now | Roblox gave 99 Nights in the Forest its Best Survival Game award (Roblox award page, 2026-09-12, RC48: build a camp, gather resources, endure the nights). The J004 trend note gives a 7-day tracker peak of 2,141,327 near 2026-10-20 and an all-time tracker peak of 14,153,173 on 2025-10-04 (IR1 row 7, S19). Our snapshot had 96,183 (DG2, 2026-10-21 03:56 UTC, one moment). The 7-day peak is far above both snapshots (108,092 in IR1, 96,183 in DG2), which suggests an event in that week: cause not verified. A second co-op night format, Animal Hospital, is named in Roblox's 2026 letter as a fast-rising new game (DG1, no numbers). Alternative explanation: 99 Nights may hold players through its horror mood and rescue story, which this pitch replaces with care and a hatch. What may be temporary: event-week spikes |
| 9 | Familiar foundation and original contribution | Borrowed (function only): prepare by day, survive escalating nights with friends around one shared camp, improve the next attempt (99 Nights in the Forest; IR1 row 7). Changed rule: the camp is a living egg; what the group feeds it decides what hatches; the hatchling stays with the players and helps on the next egg. Own expression: a mountain nest above the clouds, original sky thieves, original dragons; no forest, no watching creature, no missing children, no night-count in the title. Rights: dragons are generic folklore; no film, book or game dragon design. The marketing check for "confusingly similar" content (RC42) stays open because the night-camp structure is close to the reference |
| 10 | Smallest buildable version | Keeps the decisive action: protect a living egg through the night and see what hatches. Content: one mountain nest, a short run of nights (count TBD, game-designer, tuned in grey-box), one thief type with a clear wind-up, 3 egg foods that lead to 3 hatchlings, day gather spots, one torch-and-wall build, the hatch on the last night, a roost that keeps the hatchling. Excluded: more nests and biomes, riding the dragon, dragon battles, trading, more creature types. Dependencies: server-owned egg state and the carry-away rule (architect), thief AI (architect), egg and hatch VFX and animation (vfx-designer, animator), night lighting that reads on phones (art director), creature design (art director). Target devices: phone first (RC01); co-op works without chat through pings (RC31) |
