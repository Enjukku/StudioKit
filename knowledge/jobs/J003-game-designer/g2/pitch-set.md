# Three pitches for the next game (J003 G2)

You asked for 3 pitches and 1 recommendation. I set a budget of 40 raw ideas and wrote 40. I grew 12 of them into 13 candidates and merged those into 10. Then 8 passed the rating pass, and I picked 3 from different loops. Titles, numbers and content are PROPOSAL until you decide. The counts in each "Smallest version" and each test (zones, treasures, players) are my proposals, not measurements.

## 1. Rob the Sleeping Giant (my recommendation)

- **Fantasy:** As a tiny thief, I sneak into a sleeping giant's castle and carry its treasure home, while every sound I make can wake it.
- **First image:** The camera is at table height. A tiny player tiptoes past a snoring giant face with a golden egg as big as the player. One huge eyelid is half open.
- **Loop:** The carry-it-home-to-your-base loop of Steal a Brainrot and Kick a Lucky Block. **Twist:** everyone in the castle shares one giant and one noise meter. The rarest treasure is the loudest. When one player gets greedy, everybody runs.
- **Signature moment:** A player grabs the golden goose, and it honks. The snoring stops and the eye opens. A friend rings a bell across the room, and the giant turns. The thief slides down the beanstalk to home, where the goose lays golden eggs.
- **Why now:** Steal a Brainrot had over 25 million players online at once in September 2025 (Roblox, 2025-12-16). It had 153,037 at my check on 2026-10-21 (Roblox public game counter). Roblox named Kick a Lucky Block a fast-rising 2026 game (Roblox letter, 2026-07-30). In that game you start a danger and carry your prize home through it. The pure run-from-danger game faded: Escape Tsunami For Brainrots peaked at 4,996,032 on 2026-01-24. Its highest point in the 7 days before 2026-10-20 was 32,495, under 1 % of that peak (tracker, in the J004 trend note).
- **Smallest version:** One giant on one kitchen table, 4 sleep states, one noise meter, 5 treasures, a beanstalk up and a slide down, a shelf that pays coins, 3 upgrades. Cut: more rooms and giants, stealing from other players, trading.
- **Biggest risk:** Players learn the giant's pattern, and the fear goes away. → **Test:** a rough build with grey blocks and 4-6 players for one session. Compare trip 1 with trip 10: do they still hide and wait for the snore?

## 2. Kick the Meteor

- **Fantasy:** As the town's kicker, I kick falling meteors back into the sky so that the town survives and space loot rains down, while a meteor I cannot handle crashes on my plot.
- **First image:** The camera is behind a small player on a hill, one foot drawn back. A flaming meteor the size of a house comes down at the town. The moon hangs huge above.
- **Loop:** Kick a Lucky Block: you kick far, where it lands sets the reward, the reward earns at your plot, and you buy more kick power. **Twist:** you kick up, and the height picks the reward zone (clouds, orbit, moon, planets). Friends can kick the same meteor again in mid-air to send it higher and share the loot.
- **Signature moment:** A house-sized meteor falls, and you kick it with perfect timing. Your friend jumps through the relay ring and kicks it again. It flies past the clouds and hits the moon. Moon rocks rain over the whole town.
- **Why now:** Roblox named Kick a Lucky Block as a new 2026 game that quickly reached the top (Roblox letter, 2026-07-30). It had 7,380 players at my check on 2026-10-21. Its peak, its 7-day numbers and how long players stay: not verified.
- **Smallest version:** One hill town, 3 meteor sizes, a one-button kick with a timing bar, 4 sky zones, a plot that pays coins, one kick-power upgrade, one relay ring. Cut: more zones, rebirth, pets, events.
- **Biggest risk:** After the moon, the game is only bigger numbers, and players leave. Players may also see it as a copy of Kick a Lucky Block. → **Test:** a grey-block build with 4-6 players. Do they choose the relay, and when do they stop? Also show only the title to 5 players and ask "What will you do?"

## 3. Guard the Dragon Egg

- **Fantasy:** As a keeper, I guard a giant dragon egg on a storm-swept mountain through the nights so that it hatches into our own dragon, while sky thieves try to carry it away.
- **First image:** Night on a mountain ledge above the clouds. A giant glowing egg sits in a ring of torches. Three small players stand around it with lanterns and spears. Winged shapes circle in the lightning.
- **Loop:** 99 Nights in the Forest: gather by day, survive harder nights with friends around one camp. **Twist:** the camp is a living egg. What the group feeds it decides what hatches. The dragon that hatches stays yours.
- **Signature moment:** On the last night, thieves lift the egg off the nest. One player throws the last fire crystal into the egg, not at the thieves. The egg cracks in mid-air, and a baby dragon breathes its first fire. The thieves drop it, and the dragon follows its keepers home.
- **Why now:** Roblox gave 99 Nights in the Forest its Best Survival Game award (Roblox, 2026-09-12). Its highest point in the 7 days before 2026-10-20 was 2,141,327 players at once (tracker, in the J004 trend note). It had 96,183 at my check on 2026-10-21. Roblox also named Animal Hospital, another co-op night game, as fast-rising in 2026 (Roblox letter, 2026-07-30).
- **Smallest version:** One mountain nest, a short run of nights, one thief type, 3 egg foods that give 3 different hatchlings, one torch-and-wall build, the hatch, a roost that keeps your dragon. Cut: more nests, riding the dragon, dragon battles, trading.
- **Biggest risk:** The middle nights feel the same, and fresh nights need more creatures and art than the other two pitches. It may also look too close to 99 Nights. → **Test:** the architect and art director list the content and cost for 3 nights. Then a grey-block night with 3-4 players: does the egg-food choice change what they do by day?

## My recommendation: Rob the Sleeping Giant

- **What it protects for the player:** every trip has a real choice (small and quiet, or rare and loud) and a moment of fear and laughter with friends.
- **Why:** the carry-it-home economy runs the biggest recent Roblox hits (numbers above). The twist changes a choice and a consequence, not only the theme. The first image reads on a phone: a tiny player, a huge face, one open eye. The test build is one room.
- **Tradeoff:** its loop proof is weaker than Guard the Dragon Egg's. The run-from-danger part faded, and I have no 7-day numbers for its other references. It also needs a giant whose face and sleep states read on a small screen.
- **Strongest objection:** Steal a Brainrot may be huge because players steal from each other. My version removes that, so one computer giant must carry the drama alone.
- **What would change my view:** if the test shows the fear is gone by trip 10, I switch to Guard the Dragon Egg. If you weigh a proven loop above everything, pick Guard the Dragon Egg now. If you want the cheapest build, pick Kick the Meteor.

## Not pitched

Five ideas passed the rating pass but are not in the set:

- **Drain the Ocean (reserve).** Two open questions at once: your own pump may feel too small, and lowering that much water may be too heavy for phones. The second one can end the idea. The J004 note says fishing games have "2026 residual audiences". It also overlaps your dam game (water and a whole-server goal). I keep it as the reserve because it has the biggest spectacle and the strongest "what is at the bottom?" title.
- **Catch a Tornado.** Two open questions (reel depth, tornado effects on phones), and it uses the same loop as Drain the Ocean.
- **Babysit a Kaiju.** Young players may not know the word "kaiju", and it drops the fear that its reference may need.
- **Ride the Giant Turtle.** The title has no stake, and no repeat choice is clear yet.
- **Steal a Star.** Players chase strangers to take what they carry, and the title copies the "Steal a …" pattern.

## Decision

Pick one of the three, ask for the reserve (Drain the Ocean), or ask for a new round with a direction.

**Default if you do not answer:** I prepare the pre-production step 1 brief (fantasy and promise) for Rob the Sleeping Giant. Nothing gets built.
