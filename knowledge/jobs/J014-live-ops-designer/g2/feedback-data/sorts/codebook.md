Theme ids are `area.problem`, in problem words. Requests ("add trading", "more rewards") are kept as excerpts, not as theme names. Each theme has a definition and two examples from the inbox (short, no names, no contact details). Tagger: live-ops-designer. Second reviewer: none (single-agent test); ambiguous items are listed in the sort §E Q3.
