### F. Limits
- Test data, built to look like a game: a clean step is expected here and would be rarer in a real game.
- D1 counts are estimates (rate x new-player count). No counts by device or source. So the intervals above are for all players only. The phone claim rests on non-overlapping daily ranges, not on an interval.
- Before and after only. It could be v112 plus something else on phones; we have no test and no observed play.
- D7 and D30 for this week's cohorts cannot be read (dates in B). The stored data holds one cell the tool calls immature for each of D1 (19 Oct cohort), D7 (13 Oct) and D30 (20 Sep). We did not use them.
- The 12 Oct discovery change makes source rows (Home, Search, Other) not comparable across it. Source rows after 25 Sep only.
