# "After raising the gem pack price, ARPPU is up 30%. Great result?"

---

## Part 1 — For the owner

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **What happened** | We do not know yet. ARPPU is money per paying player. If the price rose and the same players bought, ARPPU rises by itself. That is not a result. What matters is total money per player and how many players still pay. |
| **Why we think so** | 1. ARPPU can rise when small spenders stop paying. The payers who remain then spend more on average. [S1, S2] 2. A price rise with fewer buyers can still lose money in total. Example, made up: if the payer share falls 30% while ARPPU rises 30%, money per player falls about 9% (1.3 x 0.7 = 0.91). [S3] 3. The made-up payer share change (4.0% to 2.8%, 40 and 28 per 1,000) is not clearly different from no change at that size. So a real drop could hide. [S3] 4. We have no payer count, no revenue per player, no dates and no check of the weeks before. Other changes (event, update, traffic) may also move ARPPU. [S1] |
| **How sure we are** | **Low** that this is a good result. Numbers right? Not checked: no pull. Know why? No: price effect and buyer loss are not separated. Worth acting? Not yet. |
| **What we recommend** | Do not call it a win, and do not revert yet. Check three numbers for equal weeks before and after: payers, payer share of all players, revenue per player. · who: the analyst pulls (via the lead). The live-ops or progression designer owns any price decision. · players: all players who could buy, not buyers only · how we will know it worked: revenue per player is flat or up, and payer share has not dropped beyond a limit you set · check on: 2026-10-28 |
| **What to expect** | If revenue per player is up and payers held, the price rise is good. If payers fell and revenue per player is down, the 30% is misleading. A before/after view cannot prove the price caused it. Only a test can. |
| **Your decision** | None now. Later: keep, revert or test the price (a price test is an owner action in Creator Hub). Default: keep the price and watch one more week. |
