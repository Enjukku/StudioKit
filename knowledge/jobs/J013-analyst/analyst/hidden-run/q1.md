# Q1: "Simulator games average 25% D1. Ours is 17%. How bad is that?"

## Part 1 (for the owner)

**Finding.** We do not know yet if 17% is bad. The 25% figure is a forum claim. It is not a Roblox benchmark, so it is not a target.

**Evidence.**
- Our number: 17% D1. The forum number: 25%. The gap on paper is 8 points.
- We do not know how the forum counted. Their days, their players and their game age may differ from ours.
- Roblox shows a band of similar games (50th to 90th percentile) in Creator Hub. It shows no public "good D1" for a genre.
- I was given no player counts. I cannot say if 17% is a stable number or noise.

**How sure.** Low, for "bad". Reasons: the benchmark has no source and no definition; no counts; no history for this game.

**Recommendation.** Do not change the game because of the 25% claim. Owner: open Creator Hub, Analytics, Retention. Send me the percentile band for D1 and our last 4 weeks of D1 with new-player counts. I then compare our D1 with the Roblox band and with our own trend. Review date: 2026-10-28.

- Metric contract: D1 = share of a new-player cohort that returns on the day after first play (first play = day 1). Cohort size, source mix and dates: not given.
