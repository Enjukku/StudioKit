# Q3: "Event joiners had D7 twice as high as non-joiners. Events double retention. Run one every weekend."

## Part 1 (for the owner)

**Finding.** The data does not show that the event doubled retention. Players who choose to join an event are often the players who already like the game. They would come back more often without the event.

**Evidence.**
- Joiners picked themselves. Non-joiners include players who left in the first minutes and never saw the event. "Twice as often" compares keen players with all the others.
- I have no counts and no cohort dates. D7 for last weekend's players is readable only 8 or more days after first play. If the event was on 2026-10-17 and 2026-10-18 (assumption), D7 is readable from 2026-10-26 (`calc/q5-maturity-1017-d7.json`). If that D7 was read today, it is not mature.
- A weekend event may also bring different players (new players, returning players, a promotion). A mix change moves D7 without any effect from the event.

**How sure.** Low that events cause better retention. Reasons: self-selection; no counts; D7 probably not mature; one event only. The claim may be true, but this evidence cannot show it.

**Recommendation.** Do not commit to weekly events on this evidence. Run the next event as a fair test. Option A: a random holdout of eligible players who do not see the event (needs a config key; `analytics` T-09). Option B: compare all eligible players on event weekends with similar non-event weekends. Never compare joiners with non-joiners. The owner and the lead choose; live-ops is a planned role, so the lead holds the ticket. Review date: when the next event's cohorts have a mature D7.
