# Q2: "Onboarding test ran 24 hours with 3,000 players. Early Harm Detection shows no harm. Ship it?"

## Part 1 (for the owner)

**Finding.** No. Do not ship yet. "No harm flag" after 24 hours with 3,000 players means "not enough data to say". It does not mean "safe" and it does not mean "better".

**Evidence.**
- Early Harm Detection (EHD) flags nothing until 10,000 or more players are enrolled. We have 3,000. So it could not flag anything (kit facts RF-44..46).
- EHD watches only playtime, ARPU and payer conversion. It does not watch retention, and onboarding is about retention.
- Roblox advises 14 to 60 days for a test (RF-40..48). We have 1 day.
- The players in the test joined in the last 24 hours. Their D1 is readable from 2026-10-23 and their D7 from 2026-10-29 (`calc/q2-maturity-1020-d1.json`, `calc/q2-maturity-1020-d7.json`). Today there is no retention result at all.
- A 24-hour test covers one day of the week only. Weekend and weekday players differ.

**How sure.** High that the answer is "wait". Reasons: the limits above come from Roblox rules, not from my opinion. I cannot say if the onboarding is good or bad.

**Recommendation.** Keep the test running to its planned end date. Owner: check in Creator Hub that the end date is at least 14 days out, and do not stop the test for a "win". Stop it only if EHD or a sample-ratio alarm fires. I read the result at the end against the plan's primary outcome (suggestion: D1 or onboarding completion; owner picks). Review date: the planned end date, not before 2026-11-04 (assumes the test started on 2026-10-20).
