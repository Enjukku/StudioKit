# Q4: "After raising the gem pack price, ARPPU is up 30%. Great result?"

## Part 1 (for the owner)

**Finding.** Not yet. ARPPU (revenue per paying player) goes up after a price rise almost by design. It does not show that we earn more money.

**Evidence.**
- ARPPU counts only players who paid. If the higher price makes some players stop paying, ARPPU can go up while total revenue goes down.
- Break-even: with ARPPU 30% higher, total revenue stays flat if the number of payers falls by about 23% (1 divided by 1.30 is 0.77). A larger fall in payers loses money.
- Example only, with made-up counts: 100 payers per 1,000 players falling to 77 per 1,000 is a 23% fall. The 95% interval on that gap is -4.8 to +0.2 points (`calc/q4-example-payer-share-10-to-7.7.json`). This shows the size of the check. It is not our data.
- I do not have payer conversion, ARPU (revenue per player), total revenue, player mix or dates.
- This is a before/after comparison. Other changes at the same time (a sale, an update, a different player mix, a measurement change) may explain it.

**How sure.** Low for "great". Reasons: ARPPU alone is the wrong measure; no payer counts; before/after design only.

**Recommendation.** Read three numbers together for the same days before and after: payer conversion, ARPU and total revenue, with player counts. Owner: send me these from Creator Hub, Monetization, for 4 weeks before and 2 weeks after the change. If the price is set through a Config, a proper test (half the players see the old price) gives a clear answer; the owner decides. Review date: 2026-10-28.

## Part 2 (for the checker)
- Metric contract: ARPPU = revenue / paying users in the window. ARPU = revenue / all active users. Payer conversion = payers / active users. Window and rule version: not given.
- Calc: break-even is shown arithmetic (1/1.30 = 0.769, so payers may fall 23.1%). The `stats diff` run uses hypothetical input and is labelled so. No real counts exist.
- Rules: A-03 (denominators), A-08 (mix), A-13 (compare all eligible players, not buyers only), A-12, A-15. Price source: `platform-policy` P-05 (prices come from Roblox; the owner sets them in Creator Hub).
- Competing explanations: (1) fewer, bigger payers; (2) a real revenue gain from payers who still buy; (3) a one-off effect (sale, large single buyer, event); (4) mix or measurement change.
