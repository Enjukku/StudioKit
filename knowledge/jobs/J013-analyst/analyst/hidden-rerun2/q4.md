# After raising the gem pack price, ARPPU is up 30%. Great result?

## Part 1 — For the owner

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **What happened** | We cannot call it great yet. ARPPU is the average spend of the players who paid. A higher price raises it almost by itself. It does not show that the game earns more money in total. |
| **Why we think so** | 1. ARPPU divides revenue by the number of payers. If fewer players pay after the price rise, ARPPU goes up even when total revenue goes down [S1]. 2. The players who stop paying are often the small spenders. Then the average rises because they left [S1]. 3. We were given only the ARPPU figure (+30%). We have no payer count, no total revenue and no retention figure, so we cannot check any of this [S1]. |
| **How sure we are** | Low. Numbers right? Not checked: no data pull is possible and no history exists. Know why? No: a price rise is one cause among others (an event, a new update, a different mix of players). Worth acting? Not yet. |
| **What we recommend** | Do not keep or reverse the price yet. First read four numbers for the weeks before and after the change: number of payers, share of players who pay, revenue per player, total revenue. · who: analyst · players: all players, not only payers · how we will know it worked: total revenue per player is higher and day-1 and day-7 return of all players did not drop · check on: 2026-11-04 (14 days after a clean before/after, see H) |
| **What to expect** | Higher price, fewer purchases is the normal pattern. Whether the trade is good depends on the four numbers above. |
| **Your decision** | none now. Default: keep the new price while we read the numbers. Tell us if you want to reverse it at once. |
