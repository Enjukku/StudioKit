## Locks

```text
WORLD STYLE LOCK: Stylized 3D game art with soft hand-painted textures, like a premium handmade toy diorama brought to life. Shape language: soft, rounded, chunky forms with generous bevels; no sharp points except spear tips and the edges of the thieves' wings; rocks are rounded like river stones; snow lies in soft, thick pillows. Materials are matte and tactile: felted wool and knitted cloth with a visible soft weave, carved and sanded wood, hammered copper and brass, smooth rounded stone. The dragon egg shell is thick and slightly translucent, so its inner glow shows through warmly, like a lamp behind porcelain. Palette: the night world is cool deep blue, teal and indigo, less saturated in the distance; the only warm colours in the image are amber, gold and soft orange light that comes from the egg and from flames lit from it (torches, lanterns). Lighting: the egg is the key light, a warm golden pool on the nest, the ground and the front of every nearby character; cool moonlit storm light from above as fill; characters get a thin warm rim on the side that faces the egg. Value: the egg is the brightest shape in the image, characters have the next highest contrast, the background stays darker and lower in contrast. Detail is spent on the egg, the nest and the characters; ground and sky stay simple and calm. Clean, finished surfaces, no noise, no grunge. No text, no logos, no watermark, no user interface. Not resembling any existing franchise.
```

```text
CONCEPT LOCK: Key art of a game moment shown from the gameplay camera: a high three-quarter view looking down at about 45 degrees from behind and above the players, as in a third-person game camera, wide landscape frame. Shows the player fantasy clearly in one image, with one readable focal point in the middle of the frame and scale against player-sized characters. Composed like a real game screen, not a cinematic close-up: the whole play space around the focal point is visible.
```

```text
CHARACTER LOCK: Character concept. Every character shares one shape language: chunky, toy-like proportions, only about three heads tall in total: a big round head that, with its hood, is about one third of the whole height and as wide as the shoulders; a short, round body; short sturdy legs about one head long; rounded mitten-like hands with a visible thumb; a big rounded hood that forms the top of the silhouette; a short wide cloak. Faces are simple and friendly, with large readable eyes and a small nose. Full body, front three-quarter view, neutral standing pose unless a pose is named. Exactly one character; no turnaround, no extra views, no expression sheet. Plain light grey background, soft even studio light with a warm rim light on one side.
```
