## Locks

```text
WORLD STYLE LOCK: Stylized 3D game art with a chiselled, faceted look, like hand-carved and painted sculpture: every rock, cliff, garment and prop is built from large, deliberate flat planes with crisp edges, and each plane carries a smooth, subtle colour gradient, never a noisy texture. Shape language: angular and tall, with wedges, diamonds and long diagonals; capes and scarves fly in sharp pointed shapes in the wind. The dragon egg is the one exception: it is the only perfectly smooth, curved form in the world, so it stands apart from every faceted shape. Materials: cut slate and granite, heavy cloth folded into planes, dark iron and bronze; the egg shell is like polished pearl with a molten gold heart glowing through it. Palette: deep indigo, slate blue and violet for the night and the stone; a cold white-cyan only for lightning; warm gold only for the egg's glow and the flames lit from it. Lighting: graphic and high contrast with two temperatures: warm gold light from the egg falls in hard-edged shapes on the front faces of the nest and the characters; cold cyan lightning and moonlight rim their back edges; shadows are deep indigo, never black. Value: the egg is the brightest shape in the image; characters read as strong dark silhouettes edged with light; the sky is made of large, simple, flat storm-cloud shapes. Sparse detail, with big calm areas of empty sky and bare stone. No text, no logos, no watermark, no user interface. Not resembling any existing franchise.
```

```text
CONCEPT LOCK: Key art of a game moment shown from the gameplay camera: a high three-quarter view looking down at about 45 degrees from behind and above the players, as in a third-person game camera, wide landscape frame. Shows the player fantasy clearly in one image, with one readable focal point in the middle of the frame and scale against player-sized characters. Composed like a real game screen, not a cinematic close-up: the whole play space around the focal point is visible.
```

```text
CHARACTER LOCK: Character concept. Every character shares one shape language: lean, upright, heroic proportions about five heads tall, long legs, a narrow waist, broad angular shoulders, a tall pointed hood, a long cape cut into sharp pointed tails; the clothing is folded into flat planes with crisp edges. The face is simple, calm and determined, with small features. Full body, front three-quarter view, neutral standing pose unless a pose is named. Exactly one character; no turnaround, no extra views, no expression sheet. Plain light grey background, soft even studio light with a warm rim light on one side and a cool rim light on the other.
```
