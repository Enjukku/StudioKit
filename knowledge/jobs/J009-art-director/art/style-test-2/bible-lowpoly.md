WORLD STYLE LOCK: Low-poly 3D game art. Everything is built from a small number of large, clearly visible flat facets; each facet is one solid colour (flat shading, no smoothing, no textures). Creatures are faceted sculptures with a bold simplified silhouette; trees are simple faceted cones and clusters; water is faceted planes of cyan and teal; fire, spray and smoke are clusters of faceted shards, the fire glowing. Clean real-time render with soft ambient occlusion and a smooth gradient sky. Limited, harmonious palette, two or three tones per object: emerald and sage greens, sand, slate, sunset orange, coral and deep indigo. Detail comes from light changing across the facets, never from surface texture. Not resembling any existing franchise.
