# Style test 3: same dinosaur scene, new prompting, ChatGPT web

Rules applied: prompt budget (reference-images I-11, §1a), style sets (shared text has no colour or light; each style carries palette, light, materials, what must not slip in, own framing), GPT Image 2.5 guide (visible details), one temporary chat per image (I-12).

Shared text (every prompt): "Concept art for a game, wide landscape image. A Tyrannosaurus charges through a shallow jungle river, roaring up at a huge meteor streaking across the sky; water bursts around its legs and small pterosaurs scatter from the trees. The Tyrannosaurus is the focal point and the meteor is the brightest light in the image. No text, no logos, no user interface."

| st3-clay | Style: a handmade clay stop-motion miniature set. Everything, including the dinosaur, trees, rocks, water, fire and smoke, is sculpted from matte modelling clay with visible fingerprints and tool marks; the water is glossy clear resin, the meteor and its trail are sculpted orange and yellow clay with a soft glow, the smoke is cotton wool. Warm practical studio lights like a real miniature set, slight tilt-shift depth of field. Palette: mossy greens, terracotta, and a sky of painted card in dusty teal and peach. Nothing photoreal. Framing: low side view at the river's edge, the dinosaur mid-stride crossing the frame. |
| st3-anime | Style: a cel-shaded anime film frame: clean ink outlines, two-tone cel shading with crisp shadow edges, hand-painted watercolour sky and foliage. Palette: deep jungle teals and greens, a blue-violet evening sky, the meteor a white-hot core with a pink and orange trail. Framing: dramatic low angle looking up past the roaring dinosaur toward the meteor. |
| st3-lowpoly | Style: low-poly 3D game art. Every surface, including the distant jungle, mountains, clouds, water and the meteor trail, is built from large flat-shaded triangles with no textures and no painted background. About ten flat colours: sage and emerald greens, sand, slate-blue water, a pale lemon sky and a coral meteor trail. Clean, bright and minimal, like a stylized mobile game. Framing: high three-quarter view from above the river, the dinosaur small in a wide landscape. |
| st3-blocky | Style: a blocky plastic toy game world. Creatures, trees and rocks are assembled from chunky blocks, wedges and cylinders with slightly bevelled edges and a soft glossy plastic sheen; no studs, no seams, no brand. Bright toy colours: grass green, sky blue, sunny yellow and a tomato-red dinosaur; the water is translucent blue slabs with white blocky splash pieces; the meteor is glowing orange neon blocks. Simple real-time game lighting in bright daylight. Framing: game-camera view from behind and above the dinosaur, as if the player is about to face it. |
