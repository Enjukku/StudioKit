# Hidden eval prompts (symptoms only; answers and keys are elsewhere)

For each case answer: (1) the most likely cause or causes, (2) the check that proves it before you change anything, (3) the fix, (4) what you will not change, (5) what stays not verified.

- H1. A walkable outdoor level has buildings, a hill and a lake. In play-test the owner says the ground height jumps up and down in chunks round several buildings, some grass sits higher than a building's floor, and the hill looks sunk into the ground. The owner does not want a flat map. You have the height data per column and the building floor heights. What is wrong, how do you prove it, and what do you change?
- H2. You review a finished building next to its concept image using renders from a preview tool. The concept has white trim and pastel walls. Your render shows grey trim and olive walls. The palette values in the model match the concept. What do you do before editing any colour?
- H3. A building passes every automatic check and its outline matches the approved drawing at 0.97. A screenshot from a high three-quarter view shows its porch roof as a row of separate slats with gaps. A second screenshot shows thin blue lines under the main roof. Which of these are real defects, how do you decide, and what do you change?
- H4. The owner looks at a finished plaza and says "it looks flat and I don't know where to look". The art director suggests adding more lights. What do you do?
- H5. A market street has props on every free surface: barrels, crates and sacks along every wall and on every roof. Play-testers say it looks busy but empty of meaning, and they keep bumping into things. What do you do?
- H6. In Studio a new stage feels like a dollhouse: doors look tiny next to the player, a few props hover above the ground, and the same wall panel is obvious when you walk along the street. Nobody changed the export settings this week. What do you do, in which order?
- H7. Play-testers say the forest, the beach and the cave "all sound the same", the sound jumps suddenly when they walk into the cave, and sometimes the waterfall is heard where there is no water. What do you do?
- H8. The stage runs badly on a test phone. A team member wants to delete half the props because the instance count is high, and another wants to turn ambience and effects off to pass the budget. What do you do?
