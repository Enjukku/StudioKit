## What the first test's lessons fixed (no repeat)
- Faint gradients: I computed L\* for every face before building (`refs/lab.py`); all faces step 21.8-34.4 L\*; the one weak backdrop (12.3) was found in review and fixed (spec §10).
- Short shines: every bar is drawn 20 px past both edges and clipped only by the face mask; no inner clip frames.
- f 0.294 decided up front (G2 G-06 resolved by J002-D8): all sizes in spec §9 use it.
