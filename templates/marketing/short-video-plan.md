# Short video plan: <game> · <video id> · <platform> · <date>

Workflow: `marketing-craft` (video). One plan per edit. A native Roblox gameplay video and a social edit are **different deliverables with different rules**: never reuse a voiced social edit as the native video.

**Rule of use.** "First 3 seconds" is a planning field, not a platform threshold. Length, pacing, posting time and frequency are hypotheses unless a cited platform rule sets a limit or this game's own data shows a result. No universal best length, hour or posts-per-day exists in the sources.

## A. Purpose
| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| hook | one true tension, surprise, achievement or social moment |
| audience promise | why this viewer would want to **play**, not only watch |

| Field | Value |
